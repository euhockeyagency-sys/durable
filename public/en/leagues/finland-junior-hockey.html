Finland junior hockey: U20, U18 and choosing a team

Looking for a junior team in Finland? Start with the player's age, current league and role, then compare U20 SM-sarja, U20 Mestis, U20 Suomi-sarja or the U18 route. For a family, the decision also needs a workable school, housing and season budget.

By Viachaslau Marshchonak, European Hockey Agency. Editorial update: 25 October 2026. Rule and passport review dates are stated in the relevant sections.

01

U20 levels and the U18 age category

U20 and U18 are separate age categories, not the first and second tiers of one league. Within U20, distinguish SM-sarja, Mestis and Suomi-sarja; assess U18 separately.

CompetitionPlace in the systemPublished 2026/27 format
U20 SM-sarjaTop national U20 levelSJL announced the previous season’s format would continue; check current groups and participants in its results service.
U20 MestisNext U20 level14 teams and a triple round-robin, 39 games, were approved. The winner enters qualification for SM-sarja.
U20 Suomi-sarjaBelow U20 MestisRegional first stages in September–November, followed by a national ten-team series.
U18Separate age categoryDo not compare the U18 SM-sarja label with a U20 team’s level without considering age, roster and role.

These figures describe SJL’s published decision of 12 March 2026, not a separate verification of the current participant list.

02

Age: base categories and older players

SJL’s 2026/27 age table identifies U20 with birth year 2007 and U18 with 2009. These are base categories, not a complete list of eligible birth years.

  • U20 SM-sarja and U20 Mestis allow up to eight skaters born in 2005–2006 per game; no more than four may be born in 2005.
  • Goalies have a separate rule: one of the two may be born in 2005–2006.
  • U20 Suomi-sarja and lower levels have separate conditions for players born in 2004 and restrictions on combining junior and men’s hockey. Do not apply them to SM-sarja or Mestis.

Check exact birth date, position, team and all registration restrictions. Age eligibility is not a roster place.

03

Which Finnish junior level fits the player?

EHA assessment: compare the current league, role, skating, decision speed, physical readiness and recent full-game shifts with the specific team. Points and the country name do not establish fit on their own.

  • Current role: explain regular minutes, special-team use and the standard of opponents, alongside statistics.
  • Next environment: ask about training, coaching feedback and the role the team sees for the player. Expected minutes are a discussion, not a guarantee.
  • Family readiness: school, language, housing, supervision where needed and the full budget must work together.
  • Alternative plan: decide what happens if a tryout does not lead to a place or if the player needs more time to prepare.

SM-sarja need not be the only target. Mestis or Suomi-sarja may merit consideration when the environment and role fit. Conversely, a move should be reconsidered if essential school, housing or funding arrangements remain unresolved.

Is Finland U20 equivalent to North American AAA?

EHA assessment: do not make a one-to-one conversion from an AAA label to a Finnish roster level. Compare the player's age, actual competition, team role and full-game video. The aim is a realistic team assessment, not a universal ranking between systems.

When comparing an offer, use the club-offer checklist. For a deeper sporting review, see player analysis.

04

Route for a player under 18

SJL §6.7 requires a player under 18 making a permanent international transfer to relocate permanently to Finland with a parent or other legal guardian (huoltaja), with proof of the permanent move attached. For U15 and younger, foreign transfers close on 30 November except for the stated permanent relocation with a guardian.

Do not automatically extend this wording to every trip or transfer type. The receiving club and SJL confirm the specific transfer and eligibility. A host-family arrangement does not itself prove this requirement has been met.

Confirm school, housing, responsible adults, parental consents, insurance and immigration status before travel. See the minor-player documents and transfer guide.

05

Route for a player aged 18 or older

First check age eligibility for the target league, sporting fit and contract status. Then establish the international transfer, SJL registration, Player Pass (Pelipassi), suitable insurance, housing and basis for residence separately.

Turning 18 does not remove roster or registration rules. A foreign player’s right to stay and right to perform the intended work require separate checks.

06

School, housing and daily life

A family plan needs a named school, admission conditions and teaching language. An urheilulukio or urheiluakatemia label does not establish admission or automatically mean accommodation is provided.

  • Request school and training schedules and check compatibility.
  • Record the address, private/shared room, housing period, meals and costs.
  • For a minor, identify the responsible adult, emergency contact and school-break accommodation.
  • Clarify school/rink transport and what happens if team selection is unsuccessful.

Use the parent guide.

07

Recruitment and tryouts

EHA practical guidance: each club sets its assessment schedule; a transfer deadline is not a recruitment deadline. Before buying tickets, agree the team, dates, coach, practice sessions, costs, insurance and decision date.

Establish whether the invitation concerns assessment for a team place or a paid camp. A training group does not confirm a vacancy.

08

Transfer and registration

The club and SJL establish the playing-rights history, transfer type, applicable deadlines and required documents. The IIHF stage, domestic registration, age rules and match eligibility are separate checks.

Agree in writing who initiates the transfer, pays fees, arranges the Player Pass and confirms insurance from the first training day. See the IIHF transfer guide.

For players with Russian and Belarusian passports

Russian passport: Finland restricts non-essential travel by Russian holders of Schengen visas, including transit. Defined exceptions exist; a tryout invitation does not itself establish that an exception applies. A season-long stay requires an appropriate residence basis.

Belarusian passport: the stated restrictions for Russian citizens must not automatically be applied to Belarusian citizens. Check entry, residence and sporting registration for the individual player.

Russian non-biometric passport: the recognition restriction from 1 June 2026 has exceptions, including passports issued to persons under 18. Recognition of the document does not mean permission to enter or registration with a team.

For a permanent international transfer under 18, SJL §6.7 still requires permanent relocation with a parent or other legal guardian. A resident player must check their existing status and registration history; the permanent-transfer requirement must not automatically be extended to every type of trip.

09

Immigration status

A team invitation is not residence or work permission. Conditions depend on citizenship, purpose and duration of stay and the individual player’s documents.

Migri describes an athlete permit for professional sporting work subject to employment and income conditions. It is not a universal junior route; it should not be labelled adult-only without confirmation either. Check the appropriate residence basis, including any relevant study or family circumstances, against Migri requirements individually. The sporting transfer is separate.

10

How much does a junior hockey season in Finland cost?

EHA has no verified universal season price. Ask for a written budget for the actual team, school and housing period. Distinguish one-off charges from monthly costs and identify whether the family or club pays each item.

Budget itemWhat the written quote should explain
Team and hockeyFees, ice, coaching, games, tournaments, equipment, sticks and sharpening; what is included or extra.
Registration and insurancePlayer Pass, sports cover, registration and international transfer fees; payer and coverage start date.
Housing and mealsRent, deposit, utilities, shared or private room, number of months, food and holiday accommodation.
School and travelSchool-related charges, language support, local transport, flights and return trips.
Changes during the seasonPayment dates, recipient, refunds, injury costs, early departure and remaining housing commitments.

A team place, tuition-free schooling and free hockey are different claims; confirm each item separately. Enter actual quotes in the season-cost calculator. Keep a reserve for unplanned expenses rather than relying on an unconfirmed job or benefit.

How EHA helps find a suitable team

Parents can start without a complete presentation. State the year of birth, position, team and main family question. Elite Prospects is optional; explain if video is not yet available. In the parent form, height, weight and shot can be added later. Detailed sporting conclusions may require further material.

Start with a free initial profile screening. We compare the player’s experience, statistics and available video with realistic levels and discuss the family’s goal.

  1. Send the profile. Birth date, position, height and weight, shot, current team and league, statistics and Elite Prospects if available. Add recent full-game links and the jersey number and colour. If video is not yet available, say so.
  2. Explain the goal. Target season, relocation timing, contract status, budget, school and housing needs. Add these details in the form’s “Additional information” field. Passport scans are not needed.
  3. Understand the next step. EHA explains which directions merit consideration, what material is missing and whether further work with the profile is possible.

If we agree to a team search, EHA handles the player presentation and communication with suitable clubs within the agreed scope. We help clarify tryout terms, expenses, housing and school; each option’s availability needs separate confirmation.

Initial screening does not include a full paid game analysis. The scope, price and timing of further services are agreed before work starts. Submitting a profile creates no payment obligation; the club decides selection.

FAQ

Can a player born in 2005 or 2006 play U20 hockey?

The 2026/27 U20 SM-sarja and U20 Mestis rules allow up to eight skaters born in 2005–2006 per game, including no more than four born in 2005. Goalies have a separate rule: one of the two may be born in 2005–2006. The club checks full eligibility under all applicable conditions.

Must an under-18 player relocate with a parent?

SJL §6.7 requires permanent relocation with a parent or other legal guardian for a permanent international transfer of a player under 18. This description must not automatically be applied to every trip or transfer type; the club and SJL confirm the individual case.

Who arranges school and housing?

Agree these separately. Request the school name, admission conditions, teaching language, housing address, costs, meals, transport and responsible adult. A team place does not itself confirm those arrangements.

How much does a Finland U20 season cost?

EHA has no verified universal season price. The budget depends on the team, level, housing and club-covered expenses. Request a written breakdown for the specific season.

Does a team invitation provide the right to live and work in Finland?

Not automatically. Sporting selection, international transfer, registration and immigration status are separate checks. The appropriate basis for residence must meet Migri requirements.

How does EHA help find a team?

EHA assesses the player through profile, statistics and video, helps identify suitable teams and questions about terms. Whether EHA can present the player to a specific club is confirmed individually; the club decides selection.

Can a Russian or Belarusian passport holder play in Finland?

The answer depends on competition eligibility, international transfer and residence basis. Russian citizens face separate non-essential-travel restrictions; these must not automatically be extended to Belarusian citizens. A club invitation does not replace those checks.

Does Finland accept a child’s Russian non-biometric passport?

The official decision includes an exception for non-biometric passports issued to persons under 18. This concerns document recognition, not automatic permission to enter, reside or compete.

Is Finland U20 hockey equivalent to AAA?

EHA does not use a one-to-one conversion. Age, the specific competition, team role and full-game video matter more than a label when assessing a Finnish team fit.

Can a parent contact EHA without Elite Prospects or video?

Yes. State the player's year of birth, position, current team and family goal, and explain which material is not yet available. The parent form allows height, weight and shot to be added later; a full sporting assessment may need more evidence.
