Playing hockey in the Netherlands 2026/27: clubs, competitions and import opportunities

A practical orientation. League, eligibility, education and immigration rules must be checked for the relevant season and player.

Short answer: the Dutch national senior championship in 2026/27 is the Eredivisie. The BeNe League no longer exists under that name: since 2024/25 the cross-border league has been called the Central European Hockey League (CEHL), and the Belgian federation has published a notice about its possible discontinuation. Some Dutch clubs play abroad instead, such as Tilburg Trappers in the German Oberliga. Each route has its own rules, so confirm the competition before you discuss terms.

01

League structure and pathways

The Dutch hockey championship is the Eredivisie, the national senior top competition for 2026/27 according to IJshockey Nederland. Below it are Tweede, Derde, Vierde, Vijfde and Zesde divisie. CEHL is a separate cross-border route, not part of the Dutch pyramid.

For 2026/27, IJshockey Nederland lists Eredivisie as the national senior top competition, followed by Tweede, Derde, Vierde, Vijfde and Zesde divisie. The federation also lists U23, U17, JRU17, U15, U13, U11 and U9 competitions.

Central European Hockey League (CEHL) remains referenced in the 2026/27 federation regulations as a separate external route, including rules on switching between CEHL/Oberliga and Eredivisie. Its final competition setup must be checked on the CEHL site rather than treated as part of the Dutch domestic pyramid.

What happened to the BeNe League

The BeNe League was founded in 2015 for the top Dutch and Belgian clubs. Because clubs from other countries joined, it has continued as the Central European Hockey League (CEHL) since the 2024/25 season (CEHL announcement, 24 August 2024). Do not use “BeNe League” as the name of a current competition.

06

Transfer, registration, visa and work

  1. Transfer: an international move normally follows the current IIHF process.
  2. Registration: the club or institution confirms the applicable rules.
  3. Documents: the purpose of travel must match the visa or status.

Dutch, EEA and Swiss nationals can work without a work permit. For most other nationals, the official Dutch government says an employer needs a work route through a TWV or, for work longer than three months, a GVVA combined residence-and-work permit unless an exemption applies. IJshockey Nederland separately requires the foreign player to hold the legally required residence/work documents before even a friendly match.

General orientation, not legal advice. Verify the case with the official authority, club and qualified professional.

Transfer timing 2026/27: the IIHF’s general window for international transfers runs from 1 July to 15 February (IIHF International Transfer Regulations in force from 6 October 2025); it does not apply to players under 18. IJshockey Nederland requires the transfer to be completed before a foreign player is eligible, so ask the club for the exact registration date in writing.

07

Living costs, transfer deadlines and recruitment timing

There is no single official monthly “hockey player budget” for the Netherlands. Housing, utilities, insurance, transport and food vary by city and by the club package, so get every benefit and player-paid cost in writing.

For 2026/27, IJshockey Nederland lists 1 September 2026 as the national-transfer deadline for players eligible in the Oberliga, Eredivisie, U13, U15, U17 and U23 competitions, and 1 October 2026 as the deadline to request an international transfer for Eredivisie. Those are registration/transfer deadlines, not a universal recruitment calendar. Prepare early; later roster needs may appear but are not guaranteed.

Pay: no official salary figures exist for the Eredivisie or the CEHL, and EHA has no verified range for the Netherlands yet. Many roles combine hockey with study or other work; get the full package, housing and travel in writing.

Does the BeNe League still exist?

Not under that name. Since 2024/25 the league has been called the Central European Hockey League (CEHL), and the Belgian federation has published a notice about its possible discontinuation. The Dutch national championship is the Eredivisie.

Which Dutch league should an import target?

That depends on level and status. The Eredivisie follows IJshockey Nederland rules, the CEHL’s future is uncertain, and some Dutch clubs play in German leagues under DEB rules. EHA helps compare the realistic options.

Is an international transfer normally required?

A move between national associations normally uses the current IIHF process; the club and federation confirm the exact steps.

Does a passport guarantee a place?

No. Passport status affects paperwork but does not replace sporting assessment or league eligibility.

When should a player apply?

Much roster work happens in spring and summer, but timelines differ by league.
