04

Foreign players and roster status

This page does not claim that GKS Katowice is currently recruiting an import player. Foreign-player eligibility depends on the current PZHL/league rules, the player's citizenship and work status, and the club's actual roster need. A public roster or transfer announcement is not the same as an open vacancy.

Before any commitment: ask the club to confirm the exact role, registration eligibility, contract status, work route and whether the player fits the current roster plan.
05

What a player should verify

  • exact first-team or development-team role;
  • contract length, guaranteed pay, bonuses and payment dates;
  • housing, utilities, meals, local transport and insurance;
  • equipment and stick support;
  • medical, injury, release and termination clauses;
  • international transfer, federation registration and licence responsibility;
  • EU/non-EU work and residence route where applicable.
