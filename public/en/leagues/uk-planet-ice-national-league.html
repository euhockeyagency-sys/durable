Planet Ice National League: player route and rules 2026/27

A verified guide to the top NIHL competition and second tier of British ice hockey, including 11 teams, recruitment, eligibility and transfers.

01

League position and verified status

England Ice Hockey describes the National Ice Hockey League as Britain’s second tier below the EIHL. Its top competition is now named the Planet Ice National League. This replaces the older shorthand “NIHL National” in EHA content.

02

2026/27 competition framework

England Ice Hockey states that 11 teams compete in the Planet Ice National League and that the NIHL season runs from September to April. Use the current fixtures and regulations for the exact 2026/27 schedule, cup structure and playoff requirements.

03

Clubs and choosing a target

Target clubs by position, role, roster construction, recent level, city and an authorised sporting contact. A place in Planet Ice National League does not prove a vacancy. Confirm the club, contact and exact recruitment need before sharing documents or paying travel costs.

04

What clubs assess

Clubs compare skating pace, decisions under pressure, special-teams value, role, verified recent statistics, full-game video, health, contract status, citizenship and administrative readiness. The application must show value at this exact level, not only career totals.

05

Roster and foreign-player checks

Do not infer an import limit or player category from older NIHL rules. England Ice Hockey and the club must confirm the current 2026/27 eligibility, registration and roster position for the exact player.

06

Recruitment timing and tryouts

Recruitment may continue before and during the season, but a trial is not a contract. Confirm who invited the player, dates, evaluation criteria, travel, insurance, accommodation, equipment, expenses and the deadline for a written decision.

07

Contract, pay and housing

There is no official universal salary table for every club. Put gross or net pay, taxes, payment dates, bonuses, housing, deposit, utilities, meals, travel, equipment, insurance, injury protection, termination and return travel in writing.

08

Residence and right to work

The competition level does not itself determine immigration permission. A club invitation is not a visa or right to work. The club and player must verify whether employment, sponsorship, endorsement or another lawful status is required before travel or participation.

09

IIHF transfer, licence and insurance

An international move follows current IIHF transfer rules, but an IIHF transfer does not replace England Ice Hockey registration, competition eligibility, insurance, immigration permission or a valid agreement. Confirm responsibility for each step and fee.

10

Player application package

Send passport and any second citizenship; birth year, position, shot, height and weight; current team; verified statistics; Elite Prospects or federation profile; role-based highlights and a recent full game; contract or release status; health status and availability.
