France, Poland or the Alps Hockey League: where to start a European career after juniors

How the AlpsHL point system, France's JFL rule and Poland's domestic minimum change your first senior step — with routes for five types of player.

Updated 18 November 2026.

Leagues compared · Routes by player type · AlpsHL countries · Next step

Short answer

For a player leaving junior hockey, the Alps Hockey League is often the most practical first senior step of the three: its 2026/27 rules count a non-domestic player born in 2007 or later as 1 point and one born in 2005–2006 as 2 points, against 4 for a senior import, within 16 points per game. France's Division 1 suits a player ready for a defined senior role, with at most seven non-JFL players on a 20-player sheet. Poland works best for players with Polish citizenship or a Karta Polaka, because both Polish leagues are built around a domestic minimum.

None of these is a guaranteed route. The rule tells you how much room a club has; the club still signs the player who fits a role, and the country of the club decides residence and work permits.

One league, four countries: what changes in the Alps Hockey League

The 16-point rule is the same for every AlpsHL club. What differs is the country of the club:

  • Who is domestic. Domestic status follows the club's national federation; a player with several citizenships declares one status before the season and cannot change it during the season (Game Book C §2).
  • Residence and work. Austria, Italy, Slovenia and Croatia are EU states, so EU/EEA citizens use free movement; others need the permit of the club's country.
  • Federation registration and transfer. The club registers you with its own federation; a move between federations needs the IIHF transfer.
  • Package and environment. Training, housing and the balance between development and results vary club by club.

France and Poland in short

France counts training, not passports: in Division 1 at most seven players without JFL status can dress on a 20-player sheet, and in Ligue Magnus up to ten. Transfers must be filed by 31 December 2026; Division 1 has no January window. Non-EU players need work authorisation.

Poland counts Polish citizenship or a Karta Polaka. The 1 Liga requires at least 55% Polish skaters and one Polish goaltender in 2026/27, rising every season; the TAURON Hokej Liga requires at least ten Polish players per roster and two Polish players born 2003–2010, and imports there are signed for impact, not development.

Pay after juniors

EHA estimates Alps Hockey League pay at about €15,000–45,000 per season; for France's Division 1, Ligue Magnus and the Polish 1 Liga there is no reliable sample. At this stage many offers are packages: housing, meals, a car, equipment, sometimes a smaller salary. Compare what is guaranteed in writing, and remember that a non-EU work permit may need a minimum salary the package does not reach. See ICEHL and AlpsHL salaries.

What to do this season

  1. Finish your junior season with a full-game video and verified statistics.
  2. Check your status: EU passport, Polish or Italian eligibility, French training history.
  3. Use the calculator to see whether a senior role is realistic now.
  4. Contact clubs from late winter; in France, everything must be filed by 31 December.
  5. Compare two offers by guaranteed ice time and role, not the league name (after U20).

Frequently asked questions

Is the Alps Hockey League good for young imports?

Its 2026/27 rules make young imports cheaper in the quota: a non-domestic player born in 2007 or later counts 1 point and one born in 2005–2006 counts 2 points, against 4 for a senior import, within 16 points per game. Ice time still depends on the club.

How many imports can play in France's Division 1?

At most seven players without JFL (locally trained) status on a full 20-player game sheet.

Can a foreigner play in Poland's 1 Liga?

Yes, within the quota: at least 55% of skaters must be Polish in 2026/27, plus a Polish goaltender. The quota rises to 60% in 2027/28.

What do AlpsHL players earn?

EHA estimates about €15,000–45,000 per season. Housing and meals are often a large part of the package.
