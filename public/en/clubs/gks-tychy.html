Management and coaching

Operational and sporting roles can change. Verify the official club page before approaching a named person.

03

Junior and development pathway

The local development route feeding GKS Tychy is closely connected with MOSM Tychy. The club's own current player profiles and announcements show MOSM-developed players moving into first-team contracts and roster roles. Exact youth team, birth-year eligibility, coaching staff and 2026/27 openings should still be checked with the current youth organisation before relocation.

04

Foreign players and roster status

This page does not claim an open import vacancy. GKS Tychy publishes first-team roster and transfer information, but eligibility and opportunity depend on current PZHL rules, citizenship/work status and the club's actual sporting need.

Before any commitment: obtain written confirmation of the exact role, competition, registration eligibility, contract conditions and work/residence route.
05

What a player should verify

  • first-team role, line usage and special-teams expectations;
  • contract term, guaranteed salary, bonuses and payment dates;
  • housing, utilities, transport, meals and insurance;
  • equipment and stick support;
  • medical, injury, release and termination clauses;
  • international transfer, PZHL registration and licence responsibility;
  • legal right to work and reside in Poland.
