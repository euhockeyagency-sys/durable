EIHL 2026/27: import rules, visas and how to get in

How the Elite League counts non-homegrown players, season dates, the visa route and a level check — with the season and source next to each rule.

Quick answer: the 2026/27 EIHL has 10 teams: Belfast Giants, Cardiff Devils, Coventry Blaze, Dundee Stars, Fife Flyers, Glasgow Clan, Guildford Flames, Manchester Storm, Nottingham Panthers, Sheffield Steelers. The season opened on 12 September 2026 with Challenge Cup games; league fixtures run from 20 September 2026 to 4 April 2027 (official league articles 5433 and 5442; use the league site for the full fixture list). As reported after the July board meeting, a gameday roster may include up to 15 non-homegrown players: see the import rules and what is not yet confirmed.
