# Sweden junior hockey fact-check

Checked 3 August 2026 against official sources. This is an editorial record, not legal advice.

## Verified points for 2026/27

- Svenska Ishockeyförbundet's 2026/27 competition rules define the men's U20 category as ages 19–20 and U18 as ages 17–18. The applicable age is determined by the calendar year in which the playing year ends. The same rules list U20 players born in 2007–2008 and U18 players born in 2009–2010 for 2026/27. Series-specific rules and permitted exceptions must still be checked.
- SIF's under-18 international-transfer rule requires documentation from the home federation that the player is at the highest level / nationally relevant in the home country, written documentation of a guardian or parent-appointed representative, and access to civil education or vocational training. The rule also lists narrow exceptions, prohibits an unlimited transfer in the normal case, and limits the transfer at most to the playing year in which the player turns 18.
- A player arriving from a foreign federation may not represent the Swedish club until the international transfer is approved and registered. SIF publishes 1 June–31 January as the general window for a move from a foreign club; competition-specific deadlines can differ.
- SIF describes NIU elite hockey schools and LIP local competition-sport profiles as ways to combine upper-secondary studies and hockey. Admission to a school and immigration permission remain separate checks.
- No universal SIF housing model was found. Housing must be verified with the club as a separate practical and safeguarding issue: host family, boarding, parent accommodation, responsible adult, room, meals, transport, insurance, emergency contact and medical decision-making.

## Publication note

The public pages should retain the disclaimer to verify the current season with SIF, IIHF, the club, the relevant authority and the consulate. Do not present housing arrangements, admission, visa eligibility or a club invitation as guaranteed.
