Import limits in European hockey leagues 2026/27: one table

Match-roster limits for foreign players in twelve European leagues, each taken from a regulation or a named report and linked to its source.

Short answer · Table · Who counts as an import · FAQ

Short answer

In 2026/27 the Swiss National League allows six foreign players per game, DEL2 four, the Erste Liga five and the Italian Hockey League three Category B players. The ICE Hockey League leaves up to 10 places for imports on a 22-player sheet under its 2022 rule, and the EIHL allows up to 15 non-homegrown players; both figures come from press reports of the league rules, not from a published regulation. Ligue Magnus and Poland's TAURON Hokej Liga set a minimum of local players instead: 10 JFL players and 10 Polish players respectively on a full sheet.

The numbers are not comparable one to one: each league counts a different thing — citizenship, training status, homegrown status or an import licence. Read the definitions before you decide where your passport is an advantage.

Import rules by league, 2026/27

Only rules for the match roster (game sheet) are shown. Registration quotas, transfer rules and exceptions are on each league page. Rows marked “press report” come from a named report, not from the regulation itself.

Who counts as an import

  • Citizenship. DEL2 counts anyone without German citizenship; the TAURON Hokej Liga and Poland's 1 Liga count Polish citizenship or a Karta Polaka.
  • Training status. France counts JFL status (players trained in France), not the passport, so a French citizen without JFL status can still use a non-JFL place.
  • Homegrown or domestic status. The EIHL counts non-homegrown players; the ICEHL counts domestic players under its own definition.
  • Player category or licence. Italy uses Category A and B; the German Oberliga counts import (transfer-card) players.

An EU passport therefore does not always make you a local player. Check the definition on the league page and confirm it with the club before you sign.

What the table does not answer

  • Whether a club has a free place this season: the limit is a ceiling, not a vacancy.
  • Visa, residence and work permit: separate checks (visa and work permit guide).
  • The international transfer: needed when you move between national associations (IIHF transfer card).
  • Leagues not in the table: EHA adds a league only after reading its 2026/27 rules. The league directory covers more levels without a checked limit.

Frequently asked questions

Which European league allows the most imports per game?

Among the twelve leagues in the table, the EIHL: up to 15 non-homegrown players on a 20-player gameday roster in 2026/27, according to the league release reported by trade press. The ICE Hockey League leaves up to 10 places for imports on a 22-player sheet under its 2022 rule, also as reported by the press.

How many foreign players can play in the Swiss National League?

Six foreign players per game in the 2026/27 National League, and four in the NL/SL qualification, according to the league's 2026/27 game-operation directives.

Does an EU passport make me a local player?

Not always. DEL2 counts German citizenship, Poland counts Polish citizenship or a Karta Polaka, France counts JFL training status and the EIHL counts homegrown status.

Is the limit the same as a free roster place?

No. The limit is the maximum a club may dress. Whether a club has an open import place depends on its roster for the season.
