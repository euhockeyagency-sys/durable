Poland's top division vs Ligue Magnus: TAURON Hokej Liga (PHL) or France?

Two top divisions of mid-sized hockey countries, both open to imports but counting them in different ways.

Updated 18 November 2026.

Short answer

Poland's top division is officially called TAURON Hokej Liga in 2026/27; “PHL” (Polska Hokej Liga) is its former name and still appears in searches. It counts Polish citizenship or a Karta Polaka: at least 10 such players per 22-man roster, so up to 12 others can dress. France's Synerglace Ligue Magnus counts locally trained (JFL) players instead of passports: at least 10 JFL on a full sheet, so up to 10 non-JFL. Poland's window runs to 15 February 2027; France closes transfers on 31 December 2026 with a two-arrival January window.

Which one for which player

  • Player with Polish citizenship or Karta Polaka: TAURON Hokej Liga — you count towards the Polish minimum
  • Player with French training years (possible JFL): Ligue Magnus — check JFL status with the FFHG
  • Import available after 31 December: TAURON Hokej Liga — its window runs to 15 February
  • Import who wants a full-time professional contract: Both; Ligue Magnus requires professional status for most of the roster

Frequently asked questions

What is PHL in Polish hockey?

Polska Hokej Liga, the former name of Poland's top division, now TAURON Hokej Liga.

How many imports can play in Ligue Magnus?

Up to 10 players without JFL status on a full game sheet in 2026/27.

Until when can a player join a Polish top-league club?

Until 15 February 2027, 23:59, with limits on arrivals in December and from January.
