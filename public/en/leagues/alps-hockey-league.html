Alps Hockey League import guide 2026/27: Austria, Italy and Slovenia

Considering the Alps Hockey League? Compare your playing level, proposed role, full contract package and the country where the club is based. EHA helps assess whether the route fits your profile.

Short answer: in 2026/27 an AlpsHL team may dress non-domestic players worth up to 16 points per game: 4 for a senior import, 2 for one born in 2005–2006 and 1 for one born in 2007 or later. That usually means four senior imports. Who counts as domestic, and the rules on residence and work, depend on whether the club is in Austria, Italy, Slovenia or Croatia.

01

League position and verified status

The Alps Hockey League is a multinational senior competition involving clubs from Austria, Italy, Slovenia and Croatia. Its place in the sporting system and its administrative route depend on the country of the specific club.

02

Official 2026/27 format

Official / Verified: the league’s current League Mode describes a 13-team field and 36 regular-season games per team. The regular season runs from 19 September 2026 to 30 January 2027.

The top five advance to the Master Round with ranking-based bonus points (4–3–2–1–0) and are already qualified for the playoffs. Places 6–13 split into two Qualification Rounds with bonus points (3–2–1–0). The top three in each qualification group then contest best-of-three series for the remaining three playoff places.

Master and Qualification Rounds start on 2 February 2027; pre-playoffs on 25 February. Quarterfinals start on 6 March, semifinals on 23 March and the final on 10 April. These playoff rounds are best-of-seven; the latest possible final game is 24 April 2027. Match dates remain subject to the official schedule.

03

One league, four national settings

EHA assessment: assess the sporting role and practical package together. Clubs in Austria, Italy, Slovenia and Croatia can differ in training arrangements, resources, housing and the balance between development and senior performance.

Start with the club’s country and competent federation, then establish the exact team, training group, expected role, contract and residence or employment route. A place in the same league does not mean identical conditions or administrative requirements.

04

Is AlpsHL a realistic playing level?

EHA assessment: a useful profile shows repeatable senior-game contributions: skating pace, decisions under pressure, defensive detail, puck movement and a role on special teams where relevant. Statistics need context from the previous league, age, minutes and responsibilities.

  • Established senior player: show a clear role and recent evidence against comparable opposition.
  • Junior moving into senior hockey: assess physical readiness, consistency and the actual development opportunity; a place without meaningful playing time may not serve the goal.
  • Goaltender: provide full-game context and clarify the expected workload and competition for starts.

No universal points total or league label guarantees selection. For a deeper review, see EHA player assessment. For a younger player, compare the Austria junior route before assuming senior hockey is the next step.

Juniors and young players: the 2026/27 rules give clubs a reason to use young players. A non-domestic U20 counts only 1 point and a U22 counts 2, domestic U24 players have no numerical limit, and every game sheet needs domestic U20 and U22 players. That is roster logic, not a promise of ice time. Not sure where your level sits? Start with the level calculator.

05

League roster rules 2026/27: the 16-point system

Official / verified: these rules apply to every Alps Hockey League club, whatever its country (Alps Hockey League Game Book 2026/27, section C Roster Regulations, dated 1 September 2026).

  • The game sheet may list 20 skaters and 2 goaltenders (§4(1)).
  • Each game sheet must include at least 4 domestic players, of whom 1 is U20 and 3 are U22; fewer are allowed only in provable cases such as injury or illness (§4).
  • A club may license up to 20 players per season who are not domestic U24 players; domestic U24 players have no numerical limit (§1, §3(1)).
  • A player with several citizenships declares one before the season, and that status cannot change during the season (§2).

In practice the 16 points mean four senior imports, or more when some of them are U22 or U20. The points describe roster space, not a vacancy: the club still decides whether it needs the position and role.

League rules versus national requirements

The point system is common to the league. Who counts as domestic, federation registration and the right to live and work follow the club’s country. Do not carry the conditions of one country over to another.

06

Recruitment, tryouts and timing

EHA planning guidance: prepare a current profile and recent game video before the intended move. The league calendar is not an application window or proof of vacancies. Mid-season approaches depend on an actual sporting need and applicable transfer and registration deadlines.

Deadlines 2026/27: registrations and deregistrations close at 23:59 on the league transfer deadline (Game Book C §3(5), §6). A non-domestic player may move between AlpsHL clubs until 30 December, 18:00, if the original club does not object in writing (§8(1)). After the IIHF transfer deadline, moves inside the league are limited to U24 players within the same country (§9). The IIHF’s general window for international transfers runs from 1 July to 15 February (IIHF International Transfer Regulations in force from 6 October 2025); it does not apply to players under 18. The exact league deadline date is not printed in the Game Book text we reviewed, so ask the club for it in writing.

Before accepting a trial, establish who authorised it, the dates, evaluation criteria, training group, intended role, insurance, accommodation, expenses and decision process. Paid attendance, training participation or a verbal invitation does not guarantee a contract or roster place.

Submit the profile to EHA so the level and proposed next step can be reviewed before travel.

07

AlpsHL salary, housing and full contract value

There is no official league-wide salary scale. By EHA’s approximate estimate, an AlpsHL player earns roughly €15,000–45,000 per season, not split into gross and net (ICEHL and Alps Hockey League salaries). Housing and meals can be a large part of the package, so judge each offer from the written terms.

  • Income: gross or net amount, tax responsibility, payment dates, contract duration and any conditional bonuses.
  • Accommodation: address, shared or private room, deposit, utilities, meals and local transport.
  • Hockey expenses: equipment, travel, registration, transfer charges and who pays each item.
  • Protection and exit: insurance, injury arrangements, termination, notice and travel home.

Compare guaranteed income with player-paid costs over the full contract period. EHA can help review the sporting fit and clarify the conditions of an offer. See how to compare two hockey offers.

08

Visa, residence and right to work

The law of the club’s country applies. EU/EEA and third-country citizenship may lead to different routes. An invitation, contract, registration or IIHF transfer does not itself replace lawful entry, residence or work permission.

09

IIHF transfer, federation and insurance

A move between national associations follows current IIHF transfer rules. The club must also confirm national-federation registration, AlpsHL eligibility, insurance and responsibility for fees.

What level is the Alps Hockey League?

A multinational senior league. EHA assesses fit from the previous competition, recent role, game video and readiness for the specific opportunity.

How many games are in the 2026/27 regular season?

The official League Mode describes 13 teams and 36 regular-season games per team, followed by Master and Qualification Rounds.

How many import players can an AlpsHL team use in 2026/27?

The Game Book caps non-domestic players at 16 points per game sheet: 4 points for a senior non-domestic player, 2 for one born in 2005–2006 and 1 for one born in 2007 or later. In practice that is four senior imports, or more when some are young.

Do Austrian rules apply to Italian and Slovenian clubs?

No. The point system is common to the league, but who counts as domestic is tied to the club’s country, and residence, work and federation registration follow the law and federation of that country.

What salary can an AlpsHL player expect?

By EHA’s approximate estimate, roughly €15,000 to €45,000 per season; there is no official league-wide scale. Assess pay, taxes, housing and player-paid expenses from the written offer.

Is housing included?

It depends on the written package. Confirm the room, deposit, utilities, meals and transport before accepting.

Does an EU passport guarantee a place?

No. Sporting selection, roster eligibility and residence or employment requirements are separate checks.

Can a junior move directly into AlpsHL?

EHA assesses senior readiness, role and the development context individually. A league place does not guarantee playing time or progression.
