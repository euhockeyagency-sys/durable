Optibet Hokeja Līga: player route and rules 2026/27

A source-aware guide to Latvia’s Optibet Hokeja Līga: what is confirmed for 2026/27 and what a player must verify before accepting an offer.

01

League position and verified status

Optibet Hokeja Līga (OHL) is the senior competition presented by the Latvian Hockey Federation. LHF has a 2026/27 season page, confirming the competition’s season status.

02

Confirmed 2026/27 framework

The official season page is available, but a separate 2026/27 OHL regulation was not confirmed in the LHF document section at verification. Team field, format and dates must therefore be checked on the current LHF page.

03

Roster and foreign-player rules

No foreign-player number is stated here without the season regulation. Ask the club and LHF to confirm citizenship classification, match-sheet limits, registration and the exact rule version before relying on an offer.

04

Clubs and choosing a target

Use the current LHF competition page to identify participating clubs, then verify position, role, roster need and an authorised sporting contact. Do not treat an old season list as the current field.

05

What clubs assess

OHL clubs assess skating pace, decisions under pressure, role, special teams, verified statistics, full-game video, health, contract status, citizenship and registration readiness.

06

Recruitment timing and tryouts

A trial or invitation is not a contract. Confirm who invited the player, dates, evaluation criteria, travel, insurance, housing, equipment, expenses and the deadline for a written decision.

07

Contract, pay and housing

There is no official universal salary table for every OHL club. Put pay, tax treatment, dates, bonuses, housing, utilities, meals, travel, equipment, insurance, injury protection and termination in writing.

08

Residence and right to work

LHF registration or a club invitation does not grant entry, residence or work permission. Requirements depend on citizenship, contract and club country and must be verified with the competent authorities.

09

IIHF transfer, licence and insurance

An international move follows current IIHF transfer rules plus LHF registration and competition eligibility. Transfer approval does not replace insurance, immigration permission or a valid contract.

10

Player application package

Send passport and second citizenship; birth year, position, shot, height and weight; current team; verified statistics; Elite Prospects or federation profile; highlights, a recent full game and contract status.
