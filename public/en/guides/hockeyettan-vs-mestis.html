HockeyEttan vs Mestis: Sweden's third level or Finland's second?

A big third level with 40 clubs against a small second level with nine teams — the comparison players make when choosing between Sweden and Finland.

Short answer

HockeyEttan is Sweden's third level with 40 clubs in two groups, so there are many openings, but packages vary widely. Mestis is Finland's second level with nine teams in 2026/27 and no published foreign quota: a club signs an import only when it needs one beyond its own 20 contracted players and Liiga loans. Mestis is usually the higher step; HockeyEttan is often the easier door.

Which one for which player

  • Last-year junior or third-level player: HockeyEttan — more openings and realistic minutes
  • Player with a strong second-division role elsewhere: Mestis, if a club has a clear need
  • Non-EU player on a modest package: Check both permit conditions; Finland's threshold is low, Sweden's depends on the price base amount
  • Player aiming at HockeyAllsvenskan next: A leading HockeyEttan season

Frequently asked questions

Is Mestis higher than HockeyEttan?

Usually yes: Mestis is Finland's second level, HockeyEttan Sweden's third.

Does Mestis have an import limit?

No foreign quota is published for 2026/27; the rule limits Liiga loans.

How many teams play in HockeyEttan?

40 clubs in North and South groups of 20 in 2026/27.
