Hockey in Slovakia: leagues, imports and work rules

A practical orientation for players. Re-check league, registration and immigration requirements for the current season.

Who Slovakia suits: players who can be a clear upgrade in a Tipsport liga role, or who fit a targeted TIPOS SHL vacancy, and juniors who meet the SZĽH junior age bands. Foreign places at the top level are limited and SZĽH has been reducing them, so a Tipsport liga offer usually means a defined role from day one. Non-EU players need a single permit (or a work permit with temporary residence) before working.

01

League structure

For 2026/27, the official SZĽH competition directory lists the current national senior and junior competitions. EHA links these main senior routes:

  • Tipsport liga — top senior competition.
  • TIPOS SHL — second-highest senior competition.

The 2026/27 directory also lists Extraliga juniorov, Kaufland Extraliga dorastu, 1. liga juniorov and K-Classic 1. liga dorastu. Age eligibility, groups and participation rules still need to be checked in the applicable competition documents.

04

Age and citizenship

Junior players are assessed on development potential, eligibility, education, housing and expected ice time. Senior players need a role they can perform immediately. Citizenship affects administration, not the sporting standard.

05

EU versus non-EU passport

An EU/EEA passport may simplify the employment route. A third-country national usually needs additional residence or work formalities confirmed before travel.

Important: sporting import status and the legal right to work are separate checks.
06

Transfer, registration, visa and work

  1. Transfer: an international move normally follows the current IIHF transfer process.
  2. Registration: the club confirms federation and league requirements.
  3. Residence: the player needs a lawful basis for the intended stay.
  4. Work: the contract and immigration status must permit the activity.

The EU Immigration Portal states that a non-EU employed worker generally needs either a single permit to reside and work or, in specific cases, a work permit together with temporary residence. Depending on nationality and route, an entry visa may also be required.

General orientation only, not legal advice. Re-check the current season with the club and federation, and immigration rules with the official authority or competent consulate.

07

Living costs and recruitment timing

There is no single official hockey-wide monthly cost for a player in Slovakia. Housing and daily expenses vary by city and by the benefits included in the club agreement. Before signing, get written confirmation of rent, utilities, deposit, meals, local transport, insurance and any other player-paid costs.

The main roster-building window is generally April to August. Individual needs can appear later because of injuries, budget changes or roster adjustments.

Is an international transfer normally required?

Moves between national associations normally use the IIHF process; the club and federation confirm the steps.

Does an EU passport guarantee a place?

No. It may simplify administration, but the club makes the sporting decision.

When should a player apply?

The main window is April to August, with individual openings possible during the season.

How many foreign players can a Tipsport liga team use?

A TASR press report described a reduction from seven to six foreign players per club, but EHA has not confirmed which season it applies to. Ask the club for the current SZĽH directive.

Do juniors need anything special to move to Slovakia?

SZĽH requires the legal guardian's consent for a minor's transfer, and junior categories use SZĽH age bands. The IIHF window does not apply to players under 18.
