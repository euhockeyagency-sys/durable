In short

Who he is and what he does

Viachaslau Marshchonak (Вячеслав Морщенок) is a hockey agent and scout from Belarus and the founder of European Hockey Agency, which is based in Gdańsk, Poland. He has worked as a hockey agent since 2016: assessing players, preparing their presentation for clubs, finding teams where the level and role fit, and going through offers with the player.

His main market is Europe. He also scouts European players for junior, college and pro routes in the USA and Canada.

  • Player assessment from statistics and full-game video
  • A realistic league level and a shortlist of countries and clubs
  • Direct contact with club representatives
  • Offer review: terms, registration, IIHF transfer, documents

01

Honesty

No promises that depend on the club’s decision: no guaranteed contract, visa or roster place.

02

Realistic level

If another league fits better than the one a player hopes for, he says so before any club is contacted.

03

Precision

A player is offered where the level, role, passport and budget match a real need, not through mass mailings.

04

Looking beyond one transfer: development, playing time and the next step after this season.
