HockeyAllsvenskan: player route and rules 2026/27

A verified guide to Sweden’s second national men’s league: schedule, player standard, written terms and work-permit checks.

01

League position and verified status

HockeyAllsvenskan is Sweden’s second national men’s level below the SHL and a highly selective professional environment. Compare pace, role and production with active players rather than relying on highlights.

02

2026/27 competition format

The official 2026/27 schedule begins on 18 September 2026. The regular season covers 52 rounds and 364 games. Check the league schedule for later changes.

03

Clubs and choosing a target

Do not send the same message to every club. Check the current roster, authorised sporting contact, location and whether the team needs the player’s position. A league page narrows the market; it does not prove a vacancy.

04

What clubs assess

  • skating, pace and decisions under pressure;
  • a defined position and special-teams role;
  • recent verified statistics and contract status;
  • full-game or full-shift video, not highlights alone;
  • citizenship, transfer history and administrative readiness.
05

Roster and foreign-player rules

No foreign-player number is stated without a binding current source. Federation rules apply, while the club separately decides registration, contract, roster and budget. Passport status never guarantees a place.

06

Recruitment timing and tryouts

Recruitment is highly selective. Approach only with recent full-game evidence for a defined role. State citizenship, contract status and availability. A training invitation is not a contract.

07

Pay, housing and written terms

There is no single official salary table proving the terms at every club. Obtain the amount, gross/net basis, payment dates, housing, deposit, food, travel, equipment, insurance, tax help, injury terms, trial period, termination and return travel in writing. Calculate guaranteed income minus every player-paid cost.

08

Residence and right to work

A player who needs permission to work must use the current Swedish Migration Agency athlete route. Employment, salary and insurance requirements can change and must be checked before signing or travel.

09

IIHF transfer, registration and insurance

A move between national associations follows the current IIHF International Transfer Regulations. The receiving club and associations handle the process. An ITC does not replace domestic registration, a valid contract, insurance or immigration permission. Confirm who starts the transfer and pays each fee before travel.

10

Player application package

  • passport and any second citizenship;
  • birth year, position, shot, height and weight;
  • current team, league and verified statistics;
  • Elite Prospects or federation profile;
  • role-based highlights plus a recent full game;
  • contract status, release if required and availability;
  • the exact league, role and teams being targeted.
