## Deliberate omissions

- **Budget / cost.** No fact-check contains a verified price. The page lists cost components to request in writing and shows no amounts.
- **Housing.** The fact-checks found no federation-wide model in Sweden, Finland, Czechia, Slovakia or Germany. Switzerland: the R067 training-contract template lists housing as an individually negotiated item. Canada: Hockey Canada billeting resource package. Presented as "what to ask", not as a typical arrangement.
- **Real ice time.** Club- and season-specific; no source supports a country-level comparison.
- **Junior-senior team link.** Only Switzerland is documented (U18-Elit and U21-Elit tied to National League and Swiss League club organisations). Other markets are left to "ask the club".
- **Next level.** Not rated; unsourced editorial prediction.
- **Austria** has a fact-check but is not in this first version.

## Cautions carried over from the country files

- Slovakia: do not state a single junior age cut-off (two SZĽH documents disagree); transfer regulation dated 2019.
- Finland and Switzerland: 2026/27 birth-year tables are not published; shifted years are a calculation and are labelled as such.
- Switzerland: the 2016 SIHF work-permit directive is dated and must be re-verified with SEM.
- Czechia: "no restriction found" is absence of evidence, not confirmation.
- Germany: Art. 55 window interaction and the §80 AufenthG custodian point need a second check.
- Canada and USA: immigration sections are not confirmed against IRCC or USCIS primary pages; NCDC format changes in 2026/27; NCAA rollout dates conflict.
