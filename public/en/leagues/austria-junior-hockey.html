Austria U20i/U18i: junior route and checks

Compare Austria’s junior routes by playing level, age eligibility, school, care arrangements and full-season cost. EHA helps players and parents assess a suitable next step.

Consultation for parents

01

League position and 2026/27 status

ÖEHV confirms a reworked 2026/27 youth structure. The U20 International League has 18 teams from Austria, Hungary, Slovenia and Italy. The U18 International League has 15 teams. National championships also run at U16, U14, U12 and U11. This replaces the older structure that did not include a U16 competition.

02

2026/27 format, age and registration

In U20i, the 18 teams play a home-and-away regular season for 34 games per team. In U18i, 15 teams play 28 games per team. The top six in each league qualify directly for the quarterfinals; places 7–10 play pre-playoffs. Exact birth-year eligibility, overage exceptions and registration cutoffs must be taken from the current ÖEHV competition regulations rather than copied from an older season.

03

What a club evaluates

Clubs assess previous level, skating pace, role, physical readiness, decision-making, full-game shifts and development potential, not highlights alone — the same standard applied across senior hockey.

04

Foreign-player status and eligibility

Official / Verified: DÖNAM 2026/27 §13 requires a valid licence from ÖEHV or the competent foreign federation, an approved transfer where required, timely roster registration and compliance with age and competition rules. Being under 18 or holding an EU passport does not by itself establish eligibility.

Ask for written confirmation of the player’s recorded status, any applicable foreign-player restrictions and the basis for that classification. The detailed conditions for EHÖ/EHÖ-EU status have not been verified against a current accessible registration document for this review; do not assume recognition is automatic. EHA can help identify the questions for the receiving organisation and federation.

Cross-border distinction: U20i/U18i also include clubs outside Austria. An Austrian league label does not make Austrian residence law or ÖEHV licensing the correct route for every participating club; the club’s country and competent federation matter.

05

School alongside junior hockey

EHA assessment: compare the actual school and hockey arrangements for each option. Do not assume that an academy name includes admission, language support, boarding or a timetable compatible with away games.

  • Which school accepts the player, and what admission and language requirements apply?
  • Who coordinates missed lessons, exams and travel?
  • What is included in the programme fee, and what is charged separately?
  • What is the plan if the player is not selected or changes teams?

Send EHA the school year, language level, target season and playing profile. We can help compare the questions that need written answers before the family commits.

06

Accommodation and responsible adult

EHA assessment: establish the precise address, room arrangements, meals, supervision, transport, insurance, emergency procedure and responsible adult before travel. Boarding and host-family arrangements must be confirmed for the specific programme; they are not automatic benefits of junior registration.

For the Austrian pupil residence route, the City of Vienna requires an under-18 applicant to show that a person over 18 resident in Austria has been entrusted by the parents with care and upbringing. A written parental declaration is one form of evidence. The competent authority confirms the documents for the individual application.

Use our child safety and host-family checklist when discussing an option with EHA.

07

Recruitment planning and official deadlines

EHA planning guidance: prepare a profile and recent game video before selection for the intended season. A camp, national-team selection event or paid trial does not confirm a club vacancy or roster place. Ask EHA about the assessment process for your target route.

Official / Verified, DÖNAM 2026/27 §13: licensing and roster registrations covered by these rules run from 1 June 2026 through 31 January 2027, including first-time Austrian junior registrations. No players may be added to the relevant rosters after 31 January 2027.

For a national or international transfer completed between 15 September 2026 and 31 January 2027, §13(2) generally requires participation in at least 40% of the new club’s regular-season games in the age group or a higher league for playoff eligibility. The regulation provides for an injury-related application and excludes U11 from this rule. The receiving federation must confirm how it applies to the player; a late registration does not automatically secure playoff eligibility.

08

Under-18 IIHF transfers: fees and timing

Official / Verified: IIHF International Transfer Regulations 2026, articles I.5.1–I.5.2, waive the IIHF transfer fee when the transfer is completed before the player’s 18th birthday. The general IIHF transfer deadline in article I.4.8 does not apply to under-18 transfers.

This does not waive national licensing, roster deadlines or every possible cost. Registration, insurance, schooling, programme fees, travel and any separately applicable charges need their own written breakdown. An international transfer is not a residence permit or proof of school admission.

For the family’s preparation, see documents and IIHF transfer for a minor. EHA can help plan the sporting move; the receiving club and federation process and confirm registration.

09

Residence for a non-EU minor in Austria

Official / Verified: the Austrian pupil residence category, “Aufenthaltsbewilligung – Schüler”, can cover eligible third-country pupils attending a qualifying institution. The City of Vienna describes this route for school stays over six months, including pupils arriving without their families. It requires evidence including admission, accommodation, health insurance, means of support and, for minors, arrangements for care and upbringing.

A hockey invitation alone does not establish eligibility. A pupil’s permit does not grant accompanying relatives family-reunification rights under that permit; they need an independent right of residence. A child joining a parent who already holds an eligible Austrian status is a separate family-reunification question.

The applicable route and any current restrictions must be confirmed for the family with the Austrian authority or competent consulate. For a club based outside Austria, check that country’s requirements instead.

10

Full-season costs and written terms

EHA assessment: compare hockey fees, school costs, accommodation, deposit, meals, utilities, local transport, away trips, equipment, insurance, transfer or registration charges and travel home. A programme fee is not necessarily the full family budget.

Confirm who receives payment, what depends on selection, refund and cancellation terms, injury arrangements and who pays if the stay ends early. No universal academy price or guaranteed benefits package is confirmed here. EHA can help compare a specific written option.

11

What to send EHA

Send birth year, position, height, shooting side, citizenship, current team and league, verifiable statistics, an Elite Prospects link if available and recent full-game or full-shift video with jersey colour, number and timestamps. Parents should also include school year, language level, target season, housing needs and an approximate season budget.

A passport scan or medical file is not needed for the initial enquiry. Prepare a hockey resume and clearly identified game video.

Frequently asked questions

Does Austria have a U16 competition in 2026/27?

Yes. ÖEHV lists a national U16 championship alongside U20i and U18i. Exact age eligibility must be confirmed under the current regulations.

Is an under-18 international transfer completely free?

IIHF article I.5.1 waives the IIHF transfer fee when the transfer is completed before the 18th birthday. This does not establish that registration, programme, insurance or other costs are zero.

Does the IIHF under-18 deadline exemption remove Austrian roster deadlines?

No. IIHF article I.5.2 concerns the general IIHF transfer deadline. DÖNAM 2026/27 separately sets a 31 January 2027 roster deadline and playoff eligibility conditions.

Must a parent move with a junior to Austria?

There is no universal answer for every route. The Austrian pupil residence category can cover eligible pupils arriving without their families, but minors need documented care arrangements. School, residence and sporting eligibility are separate checks.

Are school and housing automatically included?

No. The particular school admission, accommodation, responsible adult, supervision and full costs must be confirmed in writing.

How can EHA help a parent or junior player?

Send the playing profile, recent game video, school year, intended season and practical needs. EHA can help assess the route and identify checks before a commitment; selection, registration and residence approval are not guaranteed.
