USPHL Premier: playing level, selection and season costs

How to assess a USPHL Premier team, check the family budget and understand an offer. A practical guide for players and parents before travelling or signing.

By Viachaslau Marshchonak, European Hockey Agency. Updated 28 October 2026.

Compare the options · Check the budget

Assess the player's route

What is USPHL Premier?

Premier is a junior division of the United States Premier Hockey League. It is separate from NCDC within USPHL. The word Premier alone does not establish the standard of a specific team, a free season or a college place.

The footprint includes organizations in the United States and Canada. Before discussing school, housing and documents, confirm the country and town where the player will actually live and compete. Check the current official division listing rather than an older sales presentation.

What is the playing level, and who should consider Premier?

EHA judgement: start with a full game from the team and the player's specific role. Compare decision speed, skating, contact, off-puck play and shift-to-shift consistency. Statistics without opponent and usage context do not reliably identify a suitable team.

  • A younger player needs coaching, school support and a credible opportunity to play regularly.
  • A player approaching college needs a defined education objective, timeline and verified advancement for comparable players.
  • A goalie should separately discuss competition, goalie coaching and how starts are allocated.
  • Compare the offer with a European option using role, education, development and the complete family budget.

Premier, NCDC and NAHL: what to compare

Premier and NCDC require separate USPHL agreements. NAHL belongs to a different system: USA Hockey Tier II. Do not transfer tender, draft, registration or release rules from one league to another. Read the US junior league comparison, NCDC guide and NAHL guide.

How to join USPHL Premier

  • Prepare birth year, position, current team, league, role, statistics and a full game. A federation or Elite Prospects profile helps verify the history.
  • Before paying for a camp, ask who evaluated the player, which level the invitation concerns and what selection remains.
  • Distinguish a tryout invitation, conditional offer, signed agreement and confirmed roster status.
  • Obtain Premier documents naming the contracting entity, included services, full financial obligation and exit conditions.
  • Complete the official registration for the correct division. Registration does not replace selection.

How much does a USPHL Premier season cost?

Premier includes tuition-based programmes; services and terms vary by team. The official materials reviewed do not establish a single all-in tariff for every player. Hockey tuition should not automatically be treated as the entire cost of living and studying for the season.

Ask for two separate totals: obligations to the organization and family expenses outside the agreement. Record currency, months and a contingency for changed plans. Use the season-cost calculator with actual offers. Do not budget possible awards or future college aid as guaranteed income.

Moving from Premier to NCDC: verify the status

USPHL publishes NCDC call-ups and a separate Premier-to-NCDC tender list. Those records establish that the route exists, rather than guaranteeing promotion for every player. Ask whether an opportunity concerns training, individual games, a tender or a permanent place, for which season and at what cost.

USPHL Player First treats NCDC and Premier as separate agreements. An NCDC agreement alone does not require Premier participation. After an NCDC release, Premier remains the player's choice unless a separate, fully disclosed Premier agreement was signed beforehand.

Premier, NCAA and ACHA: identify the actual college team

The official 2025/26 Premier list includes NCAA and ACHA destinations. Do not turn those into a single promise of NCAA placement: a university may have different hockey teams. Verify the team level, season, enrolment and whether the alumnus actually competed.

An announced commitment does not automatically confirm a roster place, admission or financial aid. Individual NCAA eligibility should be reviewed by the NCAA Eligibility Center or college compliance office; hockey recruitment and academic admission are separate decisions.

International players, school and safeguarding

Before spending, obtain season-specific eligibility confirmation using birth date, citizenship and registration history. The public 2026/27 Premier registration page does not provide a complete universal set of these conditions. Residence and entry arrangements must be checked for the country where the player will actually be based.

For a minor, identify who handles supervision, host-family screening, transport, medical decisions and safeguarding reports. Agree a school and housing plan after injury or a team change. Use the parent guide to prepare questions.

Frequently asked questions

Are USPHL Premier and NCDC the same?

No. They are separate USPHL levels with separate agreements. Confirm movement conditions and costs for the specific offer.

How much does USPHL Premier cost?

There is no verified universal all-in price. Request a written programme, housing, food, school, insurance and travel budget, plus refund conditions.

Does Premier guarantee NCAA hockey?

No. Alumni have different routes, including NCAA and ACHA. Recruitment, academic admission, eligibility and financial aid must be checked separately.

Does a camp invitation mean a roster place?

No. Clarify invitation status, remaining selection and the financial consequences if the player is cut.

Is promotion from Premier to NCDC automatic?

No. Separately confirm whether the opportunity concerns training, game call-ups, a tender or a permanent place.

Does a US junior route fit this player?

Send the birth year, position, current team and league, role, statistics, an Elite Prospects profile if available and a recent full game. Include the education objective, family budget and questions about any offer. Tell us if full-game video is not yet available.

A free initial profile check helps identify a sporting direction and missing information. A detailed paid video analysis is a separate service with terms agreed in advance. EHA does not guarantee a roster place, scholarship or college admission.
