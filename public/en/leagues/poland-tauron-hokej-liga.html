How EHA helps with TAURON Hokej Liga

  1. Level check. We compare the player’s current league, role, statistics and full-game video with what TAURON Hokej Liga clubs assess, and say plainly if another level is more realistic.
  2. Shortlist by role. Clubs are chosen by the role the player can fill and by a confirmed need, not by a mass mailing.
  3. Presentation. A short profile, verified statistics and video prepared for the specific club.
  4. Offer and documents. We go through the written terms with the player and list what still has to be confirmed: registration, IIHF transfer, insurance and the right to work.

EHA does not promise a contract, a visa or a roster place; the club decides. Paid work is agreed in writing before it starts (see services).
