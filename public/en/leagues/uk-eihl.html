Elite Ice Hockey League 2026/27: teams, fixtures and import rules

A verified player guide to the top British league, the Elite Ice Hockey League: import rules (the homegrown and non-homegrown limits), 10 clubs, 54 regular-season games, fixtures, recruitment, contracts, immigration and transfers.

Quick answer: the 2026/27 EIHL has 10 teams: Belfast Giants, Cardiff Devils, Coventry Blaze, Dundee Stars, Fife Flyers, Glasgow Clan, Guildford Flames, Manchester Storm, Nottingham Panthers, Sheffield Steelers. The season opened on 12 September 2026 with Challenge Cup games; league fixtures run from 20 September 2026 to 4 April 2027 (official league articles 5433 and 5442; use the league site for the full fixture list). As reported after the July board meeting, a gameday roster may include up to 15 non-homegrown players: see the import rules and what is not yet confirmed.

01

League position and verified status

The Elite Ice Hockey League is the United Kingdom’s highest senior ice hockey competition. The league confirms 10 teams from England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland for 2026/27.

Country overview of leagues, imports and work rules: Hockey in United Kingdom.

02

2026/27 competition framework

The 2026/27 regular season is a straight 10-team league. Each team faces every opponent six times, three home and three away, for 54 games. The season opens on Saturday 12 September 2026 with Challenge Cup group games (54 fixtures in total). League fixtures begin on Sunday 20 September 2026 and the regular season ends on Sunday 4 April 2027; the top eight enter the playoffs.

03

Clubs and choosing a target

Target clubs by position, role, roster construction, recent level, city and an authorised sporting contact. A place in EIHL does not prove a vacancy. Confirm the club, contact and exact recruitment need before sharing documents or paying travel costs.

04

What clubs assess

Clubs compare skating pace, decisions under pressure, special-teams value, role, verified recent statistics, full-game video, health, contract status, citizenship and administrative readiness. The application must show value at this exact level, not only career totals.

05

Import rules: non-homegrown limit and roster checks

The EIHL’s roster rules do not use the word “import”. The league classifies every player as homegrown or non-homegrown and limits the non-homegrown group. These are the 2026/27 rules as reported after the league’s July board meeting:

  • Gameday roster: 18 skaters and two goalies, with no more than 15 non-homegrown players.
  • Season roster: 25 players, not counting injured-reserve replacements or, where the rules allow, two-way players. A team may use at most 20 non-homegrown players in a season and have at most 18 registered at one time.
  • Goaltenders: every team must have at least one British goaltender on its overall roster.
  • Two-way players: homegrown players under 25 on 1 August who are contracted mainly to a lower-league club; they may play 20 league games (Challenge Cup group games excluded) before they count on the roster.
  • Injured reserve: a homegrown player on injured reserve can be replaced only by another homegrown player.

For scale, the opening rosters published on 12 September 2026 show between 15 and 18 non-homegrown players per team.

This page does not classify individual players. The league’s definition of a homegrown player was not confirmed from a primary source on 15 November 2026, and the full regulations document could not be opened. A player’s status and the current text must be confirmed in writing by the club or the league before signing or travel. The limits are agreed by the league’s clubs and differ between seasons, so do not reuse figures from an earlier year.

06

Recruitment timing and tryouts

Recruitment may continue before and during the season, but a trial is not a contract. Confirm who invited the player, dates, evaluation criteria, travel, insurance, accommodation, equipment, expenses and the deadline for a written decision.

07

Contract, pay and housing

There is no official universal salary table for every club. Put gross or net pay, taxes, payment dates, bonuses, housing, deposit, utilities, meals, travel, equipment, insurance, injury protection, termination and return travel in writing.

08

Residence and right to work

A club offer does not by itself grant permission to work in the UK. Under the official International Sportsperson route, the sport’s governing body must endorse the application and confirm the required sporting status; the employer then sponsors the player with a certificate of sponsorship. The exact governing-body criteria and immigration route must be confirmed before travel.

09

IIHF transfer, licence and insurance

An international move follows current IIHF transfer rules, but an IIHF transfer does not replace league and national registration, competition eligibility, insurance, immigration permission or a valid agreement. Confirm responsibility for each step and fee.

10

Player application package

Send passport and any second citizenship; birth year, position, shot, height and weight; current team; verified statistics; Elite Prospects or federation profile; role-based highlights and a recent full game; contract or release status; health status and availability.

What are the EIHL import rules in 2026/27?

The league classifies players as homegrown or non-homegrown. A team may dress at most 15 non-homegrown players on a gameday roster of 18 skaters and two goalies, and may use 25 players on its season roster. The club or league must confirm a player’s status.

What level is EIHL?

It is the highest senior ice hockey competition in the United Kingdom.

How many teams and games are confirmed for 2026/27?

The league confirms 10 teams and 54 regular-season games per club.

When does the regular season run?

League games run from 20 September 2026 to 4 April 2027; the season opens on 12 September with Challenge Cup games.

Does a club offer grant UK work permission?

No. Governing-body and immigration requirements are separate checks.
