## Combinations with “Insufficient data”

- Austria, Finland, Switzerland: every age and type (no published club or academy season tariff found for foreign juniors).
- Sweden and Czechia: academy type (no published academy tariff for a foreign boarder).
- USA: club type; U20 academy type not offered.
- Canada: club type; “covered” type (CHL billet and education package confirmed in `canada-junior-factcheck.md`, no per-season family figures).

## Rejected

- Lyceum Alpinum Zuoz (CHF 101,100–104,900 boarding 2026/27): a boarding school where ice hockey is one leisure activity, not a hockey development programme.
- SSHL Sigtuna (SEK 474,000–570,000 boarding for overseas students 2026/27): boarding school without a hockey programme fee.
- Delta Hockey Academy: page shows 2025/26 only and states no currency.
- Aggregators (doris.school, schoozy, smapse, hockeybudget-type estimates).
