Hockey in Hungary: leagues, juniors, transfers and work

A practical guide for players and families, from choosing a realistic level to contracts, registration, international transfers and the lawful right to play and work.

01

How Hungarian hockey is structured

The Hungarian Ice Hockey Federation (MJSZ) administers national senior and youth competitions. Its structure includes the international ICE Hockey League (ICEHL), the cross-border Erste Liga, the senior Andersen Liga and national age-group competitions.

The market is not uniform: level, budget, training volume and conditions vary by club and competition. A league name is not a substitute for checking the exact team, role, contract and eligibility of an import player.

02

ICEHL, Erste Liga and Andersen Liga

ICEHLinternational professional level
Erste Ligacross-border senior competition
Andersen LigaMJSZ national senior level
  • ICEHL: the top international route for players selected for a defined professional role.
  • Erste Liga: a strong senior market with its own regulations, participants and club budgets.
  • Andersen Liga: a separate MJSZ senior level. The 2026/27 regulations set a minimum age of 16 and permit no more than three import players on the game roster.

We do not publish a single import limit for ICEHL or Erste Liga without the current regulations for the relevant competition.

03

Youth route: U21, U19, U18, U16 and U14

MJSZ publishes national U21, U19, U16 and U14 competitions and also links the Hungarian system with international U20i and U18i categories. The exact team, division, birth-year eligibility and roster route must be checked against the season regulations.

For an international junior, the club should confirm age eligibility, expected role, ice time, education, housing, insurance, minor representation, international transfer and registration. A tryout is not a roster place.

04

Choosing a realistic level

Clubs assess the previous league, decision speed, physical readiness, statistics in role context, play away from the puck, special teams and video quality. Production in a weaker competition alone does not prove ICEHL or Erste Liga readiness.

Ask the club to name the exact team and competition, explain the proposed role and show how the player fits its roster. For a junior, development and ice time often matter more than the league label.

05

Club requirements and import status

Before contacting clubs, prepare position and shot, date of birth, height and weight, citizenship, contract status, current league, verifiable statistics, role, video and availability. Goalies should add full games and contextual performance data.

Andersen Liga 2026/27: a maximum of three import players may dress for a game, and at least one goalkeeper on the entry list must hold Hungarian citizenship. For other competitions, the club must show the current regulation.
06

Contract, salary and conditions

There is no official unified salary table for Hungarian leagues. Compensation and benefits depend on level, role, experience, club budget and contract status. Before accepting, request a draft and record separately:

  • gross or net amount, payment dates, bonuses and taxes;
  • housing, deposit, utilities, meals and transport;
  • equipment, sticks, medical support and insurance;
  • trial period, injury, termination and national-team release;
  • who pays transfer, registration, visa, residence and related fees.

A verbal promise does not replace a signed document and verification of the contracting party.

07

Housing and cost of living

Costs depend on city, season and accommodation type, so one universal figure would be misleading. Ask for the address, room type, flatmates, monthly rent, deposit, utilities, furniture, arena distance and transport.

Compare the amount left after rent, taxes, insurance, food and travel—not the headline salary. If the club only helps find an apartment, confirm who signs the lease and carries the deposit risk.

08

EU/EEA and non-EU players

EU/EEA citizens benefit from free movement, but longer stays still involve registration, tax and social-security formalities. Sports registration does not replace those obligations.

A third-country player needs an appropriate immigration status. The club and player should confirm the applicable basis, place of filing, contract, accommodation, means, health coverage and right to start work before relocation.

09

Hungarian Card for a professional athlete

Hungary's official immigration authority states that a Hungarian Card may be granted to a third-country national working under an employment contract, including as a professional athlete or professional coach.

The application requires proof of purpose, a preliminary agreement or valid work contract, means of subsistence, accommodation, health coverage and a valid passport. The permit may be valid for up to three years and extended for up to three more, but each case is decided individually.

Important: an amateur invitation, unpaid tryout or sports registration alone does not guarantee a Hungarian Card, entry or the right to work.
10

IIHF transfer, MJSZ registration and insurance

  1. International move: national associations process clearance under the IIHF procedure.
  2. MJSZ registration: the receiving club checks the competition, roster, age and deadlines before the first game.
  3. Andersen Liga deadline: the 2026/27 regulations state 15 December 2026 as the transfer deadline; approved rosters may be amended until 31 January 2027.
  4. Immigration: a transfer does not create the right to work or reside in Hungary.
  5. Insurance: confirm coverage for training, games, injury, treatment and any tryout in writing.
11

Player documents

  • valid passport and proof of citizenship;
  • contract or official offer showing the club's legal details;
  • last registration and international-transfer information;
  • insurance and medical records requested by the club;
  • for non-EU players, Hungarian Card or other applicable status documents;
  • for a minor, consents, representative, schooling and accommodation proof.

The final list comes from the club, MJSZ and immigration authority. Do not book travel until the team, purpose of entry and responsible contacts are confirmed.

12

Recruitment timing and tryouts

Most roster building occurs in spring and summer, while later openings can arise from injuries, budgets or roster adjustments. Contact clubs selectively and explain why the profile fits that team and role.

Before a tryout, confirm dates, sessions and games, costs, insurance, contact person, decision criteria and reply deadline. Use the EHA tryout checklist.

13

Resume, video, offer verification and FAQ

A single package should include a hockey resume, Elite Prospects, verifiable statistics, current three-to-five-minute highlights and a full game or full shifts. State jersey number, citizenship, contract status, availability and a realistic target league.

Verify the club entity, team, league, signatory, compensation, housing, insurance, transfer, registration, immigration route and termination terms with the EHA offer checklist.

Editorial review: 29 July 2026. Recheck competition and immigration rules before applying.

What are the main senior leagues?

ICEHL, Erste Liga and Andersen Liga. Check the current season documents for format and participants.

How many imports may dress in Andersen Liga?

The 2026/27 regulations permit a maximum of three import players on the game roster.

Is an international transfer required?

Yes, when moving between national associations. The club and MJSZ confirm the process.

Does the Hungarian Card fit every player?

No. Professional status, genuine paid work and supporting documents are required.

What should be checked in an offer?

Team, league, signatory, pay, housing, insurance, transfer, registration, immigration route and termination.
