Sweden or Finland: HockeyAllsvenskan, HockeyEttan, Mestis or Suomi-sarja?

Four Nordic leagues compared by level, format, foreign-player rules and work permits, with a route table by age, current league and role.

Updated 18 November 2026.

Four leagues · Which step fits you · Registration · Next step

Short answer

HockeyAllsvenskan and Mestis are both the second senior level of their country, but they are not the same step. HockeyAllsvenskan is the deeper, more selective league; Mestis has nine teams in 2026/27 and publishes no foreign-player quota, so a club signs an import only when it needs one. One level lower, HockeyEttan (40 clubs in two groups) and Suomi-sarja (14 teams) give far more openings, but pay and employment status vary from club to club.

Choose by your current league, age and role, not by the country name. A player coming from J20 Nationell, U20 SM-sarja, junior A or a third-level senior league usually starts in HockeyEttan, Suomi-sarja or a Mestis depth role; a player with a recent top role in a comparable second division can aim at HockeyAllsvenskan or a Mestis top line.

Registration and transfer: the same three checks in both countries

  1. Sport: the club registers you with the national federation; a move between federations needs the IIHF international transfer card (ITC).
  2. Residence: EU/EEA citizens register their right of residence; others need a residence permit before starting.
  3. Work: a non-EU player needs the athlete permit — in Sweden from Migrationsverket, in Finland from Migri — and the offer must meet its salary condition.

A roster rule never replaces these checks: a club can want you and still be unable to register you in time. Start the paperwork as soon as you have a written offer (IIHF transfer guide).

Pay and written terms

Neither league publishes a salary scale and EHA has no reliable sample for these four levels, so we quote no number. In HockeyEttan and Suomi-sarja many offers are packages: some cash, housing, a car, sometimes outside work. Compare guaranteed cash after tax across all paid months, minus every cost you pay yourself, and check that the package meets the permit's salary condition if you are non-EU. The checklist is in How to check a club offer.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mestis better than HockeyAllsvenskan?

Both are second levels, but HockeyAllsvenskan is generally deeper and more selective. Mestis has nine teams in 2026/27 and no published foreign quota. The better choice is the league where you get a defined role now.

Does Mestis have an import limit?

No foreign-player quota is published for 2026/27. The roster rule limits Liiga loan players (two per game plus two U19–U20) and requires at least 20 own contracted players.

Should I start in HockeyEttan or Suomi-sarja?

Both are third levels with more openings. HockeyEttan has 40 clubs in two groups; Suomi-sarja has 14 teams. Decide by the club's role for you, the package and whether it meets the work-permit condition.

What salary do I need for a work permit?

Sweden: salary before tax of at least one third of the price base amount (SEK 59,200 in 2026). Finland: at least €1,149 gross a month in 2026, with sport as the main income. These are immigration thresholds, not league salaries.
