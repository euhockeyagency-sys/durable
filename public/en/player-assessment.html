Method

No artificial overall score

EHA does not turn a player into a single “82/100” number unless a scoring model has been validated for that use. Hockey development and market fit depend on position, age, role, league context, passport and the quality of available evidence.

Instead, the report explains individual areas, the evidence behind them and the limits of the assessment.

What we need

We will identify missing or unverifiable information rather than fill gaps with assumptions.

The report keeps source facts separate from professional interpretation.

An assessment reflects the material available on the review date. A player's development, role, health, team environment and market conditions can change.

Frequently asked

Is this the same as the free profile assessment?

No. The free assessment is an initial screening. The full analysis is a deeper standalone review with structured findings and development priorities.

Does EHA guarantee a contract or a specific league?

No. Clubs make recruitment decisions. The analysis describes a realistic range based on the evidence available at the time.

Can parents order it for a junior player?

Yes. For a minor, the parent or legal guardian should provide or approve the information submitted for analysis.

Do highlights alone provide enough evidence?

Usually not for a full analysis. Highlights show selected moments; recent full-game footage gives substantially more context about decisions, habits and play away from the puck.

How much does the full analysis cost?

The scope and fee are confirmed before paid work starts. The free initial profile assessment remains available separately.
