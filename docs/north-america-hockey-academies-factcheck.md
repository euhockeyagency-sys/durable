# North America hockey academies fact-check

Checked 10 August 2026 against each school's own published page where possible. This is an editorial record, not financial or legal advice. Tuition changes annually — every figure below is tied to a specific academic year and must be re-verified on the school's own current page before being treated as current.

Confidence key used throughout: **DIRECT** = the school's own page was fetched and read directly. **SEARCH** = only a search-engine snippet of the official page could be obtained (direct fetch blocked, usually HTTP 403); treat as needing a live re-check before publishing.

## Verified points (DIRECT)

### US boarding prep schools with hockey programs — published tuition
- **Shattuck-St Mary's** (Faribault, MN), 2026-27: boarding tuition $65,000 domestic / $73,000 international, **plus a separate $11,000 hockey "signature program" fee** — the only one of the six schools researched that itemises hockey as its own line item rather than bundling it into tuition. [s-sm.org/admissions/affording-ssm](https://www.s-sm.org/admissions/affording-ssm)
- **Kent School** (Kent, CT), 2026-27: $83,700 boarding (includes room/board, health center, technology, most textbooks); $62,900 day. [kent-school.edu/admissions/tuition-financial-aid](https://www.kent-school.edu/admissions/tuition-financial-aid)
- **Culver Academies** (Culver, IN), 2026-27: $69,500 combined tuition/room/board. [culver.org/admissions/tuition-financial-aid](https://www.culver.org/admissions/tuition-financial-aid)
- **Salisbury School** (Salisbury, CT), 2025-26: $75,450 tuition, $78,788.50 with general/tech/textbook fees; athletic equipment and travel billed separately. [salisburyschool.org/become-a-knight/tuition-fees](https://www.salisburyschool.org/become-a-knight/tuition-fees)
- **Berkshire School** (Sheffield, MA), 2026-27: $82,200 tuition + $695 technology + $895 health = $83,790 total. [berkshireschool.org/admission/tuition-and-financial-aid](https://www.berkshireschool.org/admission/tuition-and-financial-aid)
- **Tabor Academy** (Marion, MA), 2026-27: $82,100 boarding / $62,800 day; books and athletic supplies billed separately. [taboracademy.org/admissions/tuition-financial-aid](https://www.taboracademy.org/admissions/tuition-financial-aid)
- None of these six schools' own pages state whether hockey-specific costs (equipment, travel, showcases) are bundled into the published figure except Shattuck-St Mary's explicit separate fee — the absence of an itemised hockey fee elsewhere is not confirmation that hockey costs nothing extra; this must be asked directly.

### Financial aid — concrete published figures
- Kent School: average award $59,200; 36% of students receive aid; $10.9M total aid budget.
- Tabor Academy: 36% of students aided; $8.5M total need-based grants.
- Shattuck-St Mary's: ~50% of students aided; nearly $7M awarded in 2023-24.
- Culver Academies: 38% of students aided.
- Salisbury School: 37% aided; awards range $1,000 to full tuition.
- Berkshire School: ~25% aided; awards range $5,000 to nearly full tuition.
(All from the same official pages cited above.)

The academic year is not explicitly labelled on the page — confirm directly before citing. International/US rates are on a separate page not yet reviewed. [ohacanada.com/canadianfees](https://www.ohacanada.com/canadianfees)

The page itself states pricing is "subject to change without notice" and does not clearly date-stamp the academic year — confirm directly before citing. [cihacademy.com/admissions/tuition-cost](https://www.cihacademy.com/admissions/tuition-cost/)

## Not confirmed — do not present as settled fact

- **Avon Old Farms' tuition**: conflicting figures across aggregator sites ($72,300–$81,185); the school's own page could not be fetched directly (blocked both attempts). Do not publish a specific number for this school without a live re-check of avonoldfarms.com.
- **USA Hockey's National Team Development Program (NTDP), cost and specifics**: search-indexed summaries describe it as "free to play" with a tuition-free virtual school (Michigan International Prep School), but every direct fetch of usahockeyntdp.com (including the FAQ and education pages) was blocked. This must be verified on the live site before stating NTDP is free. A widely-repeated "$50,000 early-exit fee" claim traces only to a single 2017 opinion piece by a hockey advisor, not to USA Hockey itself — do not publish this figure; if mentioned at all, attribute it explicitly as an old, third-party, unconfirmed claim.
- **Victus Academy and Bishop Kearney Hockey TDP** tuition figures were found only via search snippets, not a directly-rendered official page — treat as unconfirmed pending a live check.
- **TABS (The Association of Boarding Schools) international-student statistics** ("15% of enrollment, 10,000+ students from 150+ countries") — could not be confirmed on tabs.org directly.
- **Culver Academies' published alumni commitment list** — confirmed to exist as a URL via search, but its content was not directly read; treat any specific names/numbers from it as unconfirmed until read directly.

## Structural distinction to keep clear in the guide

Three genuinely different pathways exist and must not be conflated:
1. **CHL (Canada)** — already covered on `leagues/canada-junior-hockey.html`. A club invites the player via the Import Draft; billeting plus a league-funded tuition-and-books package, not a family-paid private school.
2. **NCDC (USA)** — already covered on `leagues/usa-ncdc.html`. A USPHL junior league; "tuition-free" refers to hockey-programme fees only, and academic schooling is arranged individually per player/family, not a bundled prep-school product.
3. **Private hockey academies / prep schools (this guide)** — the family pays a private school directly for a bundled academic-plus-hockey boarding (or day) programme, independent of any club invitation. This is the only one of the three where a specific, large, published annual price tag exists.

USA Hockey's NTDP is a fourth, distinct thing again: a free, elite, invitation-only national team programme, not a private pay-to-play academy — its specifics need the live-site re-check noted above, but the *category* distinction (selective/free vs. pay-to-attend) is safe to state.

## Publication note

Every dollar figure used on the public page must carry its academic year and source school inline (not just in a footnote), since these figures will read very differently to a family a year or two after publication if presented without a date. Do not compute or imply an "average" cost across schools — present the six US figures and two Canadian figures as individual, named, dated data points, not as a statistic. Do not state a cost or the "free" claim for NTDP without either sourcing it properly on the page as unconfirmed or leaving it as a structural description only. Do not use the uncredited "93 NHL draft picks" style aggregator figures.
