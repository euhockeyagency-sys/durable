1. Līga: player route and rules 2026/27

A verified guide to Latvia’s 1. Līga: 2026/27 age eligibility, foreign-player rule, roster administration and the player route.

01

League position and verified status

Latvia’s 1. Līga is a senior championship organised by LHF. The published 2026/27 regulations govern the competition and define player eligibility, roster administration and foreign-player use.

02

Confirmed 2026/27 framework

The championship runs from September 2026 to the end set by the approved schedule. Players born in 2010 or earlier may participate; younger players require an exceptional LHF Coaches Committee permission.

03

Roster and foreign-player rules

A team may register and use no more than five foreign players. Latvian, Lithuanian, Estonian and Ukrainian citizens are not counted in that five-player limit. Classification must still be confirmed by LHF.

04

Clubs and choosing a target

Use current LHF records to identify clubs, then verify position, role, roster need and an authorised contact. A team’s participation does not prove a vacancy, pay level or housing offer.

05

What clubs assess

Clubs compare skating, decisions, physical readiness, role, special teams, verified statistics, full-game video, age, health, contract status, citizenship and registration readiness.

06

Recruitment timing and tryouts

After 1 February 2027 at 17:00, the final roster may not exceed 30 players and further roster changes are not allowed under the published rule. A trial must state dates, costs, insurance and decision timing.

07

Contract, pay and housing

There is no official universal salary table for all 1. Līga clubs. Put pay, taxes, payment dates, housing, utilities, meals, travel, equipment, insurance, injury protection and termination in writing.

08

Residence and right to work

League eligibility and LHF registration do not grant entry, residence or work permission. Requirements depend on citizenship, age and contract and must be checked with the competent Latvian authorities.

09

IIHF transfer, licence and insurance

An international move follows current IIHF transfer rules plus LHF licensing and competition eligibility. Transfer approval does not replace health insurance, immigration permission or a valid agreement.

10

Player application package

Send passport and second citizenship; birth year, position, shot, height and weight; current team; verified statistics; Elite Prospects or federation profile; highlights, a recent full game and contract status.
