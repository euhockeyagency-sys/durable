Levels and age categories

U20, U18 and U16 are reader-friendly approximations here; ČSLH regulations use the Czech category names. Extraliga juniorů and Extraliga staršího dorostu are different age competitions, not the first and second tiers of one league.

02

Age: base category and exceptions

ČSLH’s 2026/27 base categories are junioři: 2007–2008; starší dorostenci: 2009–2010; mladší dorostenci: 2011. Younger-player and overage eligibility depends on the specific competition rules.

Extraliga juniorů and Liga juniorů separately limit participation by players born in 2009–2010 and provide conditional exceptions for 2006-born players linked to prior registration and playing history. Being born in 2006 alone does not make a new international player eligible. The club checks the full rules, position and player history.

03

Foreign players and goalies

Important restriction: technical rules, item 4 for Extraliga juniorů and Liga juniorů and item 3 for CCM Extraliga staršího dorostu, allow at most two players in the defined Cizí státní příslušník category per game and prohibit their participation as goalies.

The category is defined through points b) and d) of the relevant ČSLH transfer regulations. Do not infer status from a passport alone: the receiving club and federation must confirm it. Regional and other age competitions require their own rules; a tryout place does not establish eligibility.

04

How to assess a suitable level

EHA assessment: compare the current league, role, skating, decision speed, physical readiness and full shifts with the specific team. Establish eligibility first, then sporting fit.

A Liga or regional team may merit consideration if training, role and terms fit the player. The Extraliga name does not guarantee development, ice time or a professional contract.

05

International transfer under 18

IIHF’s 2026 regulations, I.5, provide for under-18 transfers through the transfer system. The general IIHF deadline in I.4.8 does not apply; a transfer completed before the 18th birthday is exempt from the transfer fee under I.5.1. This does not remove other costs or domestic restrictions.

The club and ČSLH confirm playing-rights history, consents, documents, registration and eligibility for the competition. Also agree school, housing, adult responsibility and insurance before travel.

06

School and language

Check the specific school and hockey programme. A team invitation does not confirm admission, English-language teaching, timetable compatibility or a dormitory place.

  • School, programme, admission documents and deadlines.
  • Teaching language and support for international students.
  • Travel to the rink, exams and match-related absence.
  • School arrangements if selection fails or the player changes teams.
07

Housing and safeguarding

Separately confirm the address, room, dates, meals, deposit, transport and holiday accommodation. For a minor, document the responsible adult, emergency contact and parental consents. School dormitory accommodation is not automatically included in a hockey programme.

08

Tryouts, registration and timing

Before buying tickets, agree the age category, team, coach, training dates, payment, insurance and decision date. Distinguish a commercial camp from assessment for a roster place.

Transfer, domestic registration and later-stage eligibility are separate checks. Technical regulations include games-played requirements and exceptions, including conditions linked to 31 December 2026. A late transfer does not automatically provide playoff or qualification eligibility. ČSLH confirms the player-specific deadlines.

09

Residence: sport and education

The Czech Interior Ministry describes the long-term sports visa for professional activity in professional competitions with the required remuneration; amateur competitions are excluded from that basis. It cannot be promised to any junior with a team invitation.

For an education route, establish whether the programme qualifies as studies under §64 or as other educational activities. The school, Ministry and consulate confirm the appropriate basis for the player’s citizenship, age and documents. Transfer and immigration status remain separate.

10

Season budget and written terms

This review has no verified universal price. Obtain a club-specific CZK budget for hockey, school, housing, meals, transport, insurance, equipment and travel. EHA service terms are agreed separately.

  • Club fees: ice, coaching, games and tournaments.
  • School, housing, deposit, utilities and meals.
  • Transport, flights, equipment, insurance and medical costs.
  • Registration, transfer-related costs and the payer for each item.
  • Refunds, injury, unsuccessful selection and early departure.

Record the amount, currency, period and payment recipient. If the club covers some expenses, state the remaining family contribution separately.

FAQ

Which birth years are U20, U18 and U16?

ČSLH’s 2026/27 base categories are junioři: 2007–2008; starší dorostenci: 2009–2010; mladší dorostenci: 2011. Younger-player and overage eligibility depends on the specific competition rules.

Can a foreign goalie play Czech junior hockey?

First establish the player’s status under ČSLH rules. Extraliga juniorů, Liga juniorů and CCM Extraliga staršího dorostu prohibit players in the Cizí státní příslušník category defined by the cited regulations from playing goalie. Do not automatically apply this to other competitions.

How much does a season in Czechia cost?

This review has no verified universal price. Obtain a club-specific CZK budget for hockey, school, housing, meals, transport, insurance, equipment and travel. EHA service terms are agreed separately.

Does a team place include school and housing?

These are separate arrangements. Confirm admission, teaching language, housing address, meals, transport and the responsible adult in writing.

Is an invitation sufficient to relocate?

An invitation does not replace competition eligibility, international transfer or an appropriate residence basis. Check these separately before travel.

How do I start an EHA team assessment?

Send birth date, position, citizenship, current team and league, statistics, a full game, contract status, goal and budget. Suitable levels and further work are discussed after profile assessment; the club decides selection.
