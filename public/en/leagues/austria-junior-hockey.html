Austria U20i/U18i: junior route and checks

A practical, cautious overview of Austria's upper junior route for a player and family. The U20 and U18 International Leagues run cross-border, while Austria also has a national U16 championship in 2026/27.

01

League position and 2026/27 status

ÖEHV confirms a reworked 2026/27 youth structure. The U20 International League has 18 teams from Austria, Hungary, Slovenia and Italy. The U18 International League has 15 teams. National championships also run at U16, U14, U12 and U11. This replaces the older structure that did not include a U16 competition.

02

2026/27 format, age and registration

In U20i, the 18 teams play a home-and-away regular season for 34 games per team. In U18i, 15 teams play 28 games per team. The top six in each league qualify directly for the quarterfinals; places 7–10 play pre-playoffs. Exact birth-year eligibility, overage exceptions and registration cutoffs must be taken from the current ÖEHV competition regulations rather than copied from an older season.

03

What a club evaluates

Clubs assess previous level, skating pace, role, physical readiness, decision-making, full-game shifts and development potential, not highlights alone — the same standard applied across senior hockey.

04

Foreign players under 18 — Austria's own condition

Austria does not have a Finland-style rule requiring a guardian to permanently relocate as a precondition of a transfer. Instead, ÖEHV uses a status mechanism called Eishockeyösterreicher (EHÖ): a non-Austrian player licensed with ÖEHV clubs for five consecutive seasons before turning 18 is reclassified as domestic rather than a transfer player. A faster route, EHÖ-EU, applies only to EU-citizen players whose family relocated to Austria before the player's 17th birthday, with at least one year of residence and a parent under Austrian social insurance. Neither status is a gate: a family that does not relocate can still complete an ordinary international transfer and get the player licensed — they simply remain classified as an import for club roster-quota and fee-discount purposes, not for eligibility itself.

Separately, ÖEHV's registration rules set domestic licensing periods that must be checked alongside IIHF transfer rules. For U20i/U18i, the exact roster-addition deadline must be taken from the current DÖNAM 2026/27 rather than copied from an older season. The club should confirm the applicable Austrian registration deadline before any move is booked.

Underneath both of these Austrian rules, the IIHF baseline applies: no transfer fee before age 18, and the general 1 July–15 February transfer-deadline window does not apply to under-18 transfers.

05

Education alongside hockey

Austria's federally recognised dual-career sport-school network is built around skiing (the Skihauptschule/Skimittelschule system) — there is no equivalent nationally codified hockey-school system. Instead, hockey education support runs through named, city-specific club academies, each brokering its own school partnership: Eishockey-Akademie Oberösterreich in Linz (with EV Linz, a partner school and a student dormitory; admission is roughly after the winter semester break), Eishockey-Akademie Steiermark in Graz (tied historically to a local dual-career school; the current partnership should be confirmed directly), and Red Bull Salzburg's internal academy system. Arrangements vary and must be confirmed directly with the specific academy.

06

Housing and care

No ÖEHV federation-level housing or billeting programme was found. The Linz academy bundles a dormitory into its own package; this was not confirmed for the Graz or Salzburg academies. As with every junior market on this site, housing must be confirmed with the specific club or academy as a separate practical and safeguarding matter: dormitory, host family or boarding arrangement, responsible adult, room, meals, transport, insurance and emergency contact.

07

Recruitment timing

No ÖEHV document states an official league-wide tryout window. ÖEHV runs national-team talent-identification camps for U14/U15 players, but these are national-team selection camps, not club recruitment tryouts. The Linz academy's own admission process runs roughly after the winter semester break. Request a real assessment schedule, evaluation criteria and decision date directly from the club or academy.

08

Transfer and registration

If the player was registered abroad, the move requires both a standard IIHF international transfer and ÖEHV registration, plus a valid passport (or birth certificate and citizenship proof for a player without one) and a passport photo. The club and ÖEHV must confirm the exact process, and — separately from eligibility — whether the player's status will be recorded as EHÖ, EHÖ-EU, or an ordinary transfer/import for roster-quota purposes.

09

Immigration and travel

Austria is EU/Schengen; standard EU/EEA-vs-non-EU rules apply. For a non-EU minor, there is no dedicated athlete visa. Two general routes apply: family reunification, which requires the sponsoring parent to already hold a qualifying Austrian residence status (a suspension on some new applications is in effect through 30 September 2026 — re-check before relying on this); or the "Aufenthaltsbewilligung – Schüler" pupil residence permit, which does not require a parent to relocate — an adult in Austria (which can be a host family, school director or boarding-school staff) must be formally entrusted with the player's care via a written parental declaration, alongside school enrolment, accommodation and insurance proof. This is general immigration law, not a hockey-specific rule, and must be confirmed with the embassy, consulate or an immigration professional before travel.

10

Costs and written terms

Do not rely on verbal promises about fees, equipment, housing or travel. Ask for a written breakdown, payment recipient, refund terms and what is conditional on selection.

11

Player package

Send year of birth, position, height, citizenship, current team, league, statistics, Elite Prospects or federation profile, highlights and a recent full game.
