# Country pages: fact-check notes

## Poland: right to work for non-EU players (checked 21 November 2026)

Used in the `#visa-work` section of `guides/hockey-in-poland` and `ru/guides/hokkej-v-polshe`.

- **DIRECT (search copy of official text)**, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1620, regulation of the Minister of Family, Labour and Social Policy of 20 November 2025 (gov.pl attachment e6c2117b…): a work permit is not required for an athlete working for a sports club based in Poland for up to 30 days in a calendar year. In force from 1 December 2025 (Zielona Linia, zielonalinia.gov.pl).
- **SEARCH (official sites)**, Zielona Linia and Powiatowy Urząd Pracy (praca.gov.pl, March 2026): the declaration on entrusting work (oświadczenie) applies from 1 December 2025 only to citizens of Armenia, Belarus, Moldova and Ukraine; Georgia was removed; up to 24 months per declaration; filed by the Polish employer via praca.gov.pl. Ukrainians with temporary protection can also work on a notification (powiadomienie).
- **SEARCH (official site)**, Wielkopolski Urząd Wojewódzki (migrant.poznan.uw.gov.pl): a holder of a valid Pole's Card is exempt from a work permit; employing a foreigner otherwise needs a document legalising the work; since 1 June 2025 work-permit applications go only through praca.gov.pl. Direct fetch of this site failed (robots/connection); facts taken from the indexed official page text.
- Not stated on the page: permit fees, processing times, salary thresholds. Russia and Kazakhstan are named only as examples of countries outside the declaration list.
