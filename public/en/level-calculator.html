    Calculator questions

    How should I use the calculator result?

    Use it as an initial shortlist for research. The model uses your current league category, scoring relative to position, age and seasons played. It does not assess video, verify your statistics or measure the strength of every league.

    Does the calculator guarantee a contract?

    No. The result does not confirm vacancies, interest from clubs or eligibility. A personal review of your video and statistics, followed by checks with the relevant club and federation, is needed.

    Does an EU passport remove import limits?

    Not automatically. Import classification depends on the competition rules and the player's individual status. Permission to work, registration and international transfer requirements are separate checks. The calculator's passport filter is a broad model assumption, not an eligibility decision.
