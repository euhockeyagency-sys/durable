# Five league pages: fact-check (17 November 2026)

Pages: France Division 1, Sweden Hockeyettan, Alps Hockey League, Poland TAURON Hokej Liga, CEHL (Netherlands / former BeNe League), EN and RU. All ten pages already existed; this review added an "How EHA helps" section with next-step links on each, and the facts below. Earlier facts on these pages (verified 2 October 2026) were not re-checked unless listed.

## CEHL and the BeNe League

- CEHL, "CEHL 2024-2025 competitie opzet" (https://www.cehl.eu/node/4682): 9 clubs from the last weekend of September 2024 (The Hague, Geleen, Heerenveen; Diez, Neuwied; Herentals, Leuven, Liège, Mechelen). Read from a search extract on 17 November 2026.
- CEHL stats page (https://www.cehl.eu/leagues/cehl/stats): shows the 2025-2026 season with 8 clubs (EHC Die Bären Neuwied, Heylen Vastgoed HYC, Liège Bulldogs, Mechelen Golden Sharks, Snackpoint Eaters Limburg, Tout Bien Leuven Chiefs, UltimAir Hijs Hokij, Unis Flyers Heerenveen). No 2026/27 list on that page.
- DEB press release, 1 July 2026 (https://www.deb-online.de/en/2026/07/01/deb-oberligen-zulassungsverfahren-fuer-die-saison-2026-2027-abgeschlossen/): EHC Neuwied, reigning CEHL champion, joins the Oberliga Nord for 2026/27.
- The rename from BeNe League to CEHL for 2024/25 is stated by Wikipedia ("2024–25 Central European Hockey League season"), a secondary source; the CEHL 2024/25 setup above is consistent with it.
- Not confirmed, not published: the 2026/27 CEHL club list and format. The page keeps the statement that the final setup must be checked on the live CEHL site.

## TAURON Hokej Liga: Polish-player minimum

- Flashscore.pl season-launch report (https://www.flashscore.pl/wiadomosci/10-druzyn-i-wysokie-nagrody-w-systemie-juniorskim-startuje-sezon-tauron-hokej-ligi/UFdJtiId/), quoting PZHL president Krzysztof Woźniak: Polish players in the match roster had to number 8 last season, 10 for the next two seasons, then 11 and 12. The article does not name the season, but it describes the 10-team league with Nowy Targ and Sanok returning, which matches the 2026/27 schedule already cited on the page.
- Polishpuck (17 April 2026) also mentions a "10 Pole minimum" for the 2026-27 season.
- Not confirmed: the wording in the current PZHL regulation (polskihokej.eu could not be opened in this session). The pages label the rule as a press report and say it is a minimum for Polish players, not a cap on imports.
