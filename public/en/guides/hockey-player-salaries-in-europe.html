Ice hockey salaries in Europe: pay by league and contract terms

There is no single European ice hockey salary. A useful comparison needs the league, player's role, currency, gross or net basis, paid months and housing terms. A published minimum, a previous year's taxable income and an agency estimate answer different questions.

This guide covers ice hockey. The evidence below spans different years; it is not a verified salary scale for every league in 2026/27.

What the sources actually establish

SHL: historical minimums and taxable income

SICO's current player page links collective agreements for SHL and HockeyAllsvenskan. The linked SHL PDF, updated 11 May 2020, records monthly rookie amounts of SEK 22,000 at age 18 to SEK 25,000 at age 21, effective from 1 May 2019. It also provides for annual adjustments. These are historical amounts, not a verified 2026/27 minimum.

The third-party site hockeyarena.se, updated 25 September 2026, reports an average of SEK 842,960 for 2025 taxable income, excluding players without recorded income. That is the site's calculation from a prior tax year, not an official current league salary average or take-home pay.

HockeyAllsvenskan and Hockeyettan

The old undated SICO figures previously cited here could not be reverified on the current player page and have been removed. For HockeyAllsvenskan, check the applicable collective agreement and written offer. For Hockeyettan, an external high-end import estimate cannot establish typical pay for all players. Compare the HockeyAllsvenskan and Hockeyettan guides for league context.

Liiga

Yle's report of 21 January 2026 describes rising offers for specific players. It reports a net contract below €200,000 for Patrick Curry and an employer cost around €400,000 after taxes and charges. This illustrates why net pay and a club's total cost must not be compared as if they were the same salary. It is reporting, not a league-wide pay table.

ICEHL, DEL2 and AlpsHL

The ranges in the table come from Play Hockey in Europe's article dated 7 September 2026, reviewed on 25 October 2026. The author describes them as network- and player-based estimates. Their presence in this guide does not independently confirm a current offer. Use the ICEHL, DEL2 and Alps Hockey League guides alongside the contract details.

Czechia, Germany, Switzerland and other markets

A current official individual salary table is not established by the sources cited here. The same applies to Poland, Slovakia, Norway, Denmark and Hungary. External estimates do exist, including for Slovakia; their existence does not verify pay for a particular player. Specify the actual competition and club, then check the written terms. How to compare two club offers

EHA practical checklist. Compare the same paid period and only the guaranteed terms of a written offer.

  1. Cash: currency, gross or net pay, paid months, payment dates, deductions and tax responsibility.
  2. Housing: who pays rent, deposit and utilities; a private room or shared accommodation.
  3. Other costs: food, transport, flights, equipment, insurance, registration and transfer.
  4. Risks: injury pay, trial conditions, termination and return travel. Keep conditional bonuses separate.

Cash remaining over the paid period = guaranteed cash after tax − player-paid costs over that same period. Show club-paid housing as a separate benefit; do not add its value to cash in hand. Do not budget for outside employment until the right to do that work is confirmed.

Parents: a junior pathway has a different budget

Do not use a senior professional's salary as the budget for a junior placement. First establish whether the offer is a paid contract, expense support or a programme with family-paid fees. School, housing, meals, insurance and travel need their own written breakdown.

NHL, AHL and ECHL salaries: a separate comparison

The NHLPA FAQ lists the NHL minimum at US$850,000 for 2026/27. This is an NHL minimum, not a European salary benchmark. Older AHL and ECHL minimums previously quoted on this page have been removed to avoid presenting an expired season's figures as current. Use the applicable league agreement and season; a team payroll cap is not one player's wage.

Salary questions from players and parents

How much do ICEHL players make?

An external agency article dated 7 September 2026 gives a €40,000–€150,000+ range. This is not an official pay scale. Confirm the period, gross or net basis, paid months and benefits in the club's written offer.

What is the average HockeyAllsvenskan salary?

A current official average is not verified in this guide. The external estimate shown is not a league minimum or a typical-player guarantee.

Are European hockey salaries gross or net?

There is no universal convention. Ask for the currency, gross or net basis, tax responsibility, paid months, deductions and payment dates in writing.

Does a hockey salary include housing?

Not automatically. Confirm rent, deposits, utilities, room sharing and whether the housing arrangement ends if the player is released.

Do junior players receive a salary?

Establish the exact offer: a paid contract, expense support and a family-funded programme are different arrangements. Do not apply senior salary figures to a junior season.
