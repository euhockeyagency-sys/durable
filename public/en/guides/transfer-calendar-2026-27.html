Hockey transfer calendar 2026/27: international transfers and league deadlines

All 28 countries of the league directory, filterable by country, league, age, deadline month and “open now”, with the official source and check date for each row.

Short answer

For season 2026/27 the IIHF window for international transfers of adult players runs from 1 July 2026 to 15 February 2027, but each league sets its own registration deadline inside it — for example 30 December 2026 for non-domestic changes in the Alps Hockey League, 31 December 2026 in France, 11 January 2027 in Italy's second level and 15 February 2027 in DEL2, Poland's top league and Switzerland's National League (foreign players). The table covers every country in EHA's league directory; where no date was confirmed, it says so.

Calendar, season 2026/27

Filter by country, league, age, deadline month or “Open now”; the January and February buttons show the deadlines of those months. Types: international transfer (the IIHF or federation window for a move between countries — see the IIHF transfer guide), league registration / late additions (the last day a club can register or add a player), domestic transfer window and other restrictions (for example limits on arrivals). Junior and senior rules are separate rows; “All ages” rows apply to both.

Status: Confirmed — read in the named official document; Requires checking — a date exists but EHA has not confirmed it for 2026/27 (dates from the previous season are not carried over); Date not found in published sources — we looked and found none. This is different from Window closed, which means a confirmed window has already ended. “Open now” lists only windows whose start and end are both confirmed; a future deadline without a confirmed start is shown as “deadline ahead”. Where the source gives no time or time zone, none is added. Coverage on 19 November 2026: 28 countries, 10 of them with at least one confirmed date, plus the IIHF window.

Know the deadline — now check which level fits you. Take the EHA Level Calculator: it gives an indicative league range in two minutes. It does not check citizenship, age rules or registration, so treat the result as preliminary.

How to use the calendar

  • A deadline is the last day for paperwork, not the time clubs look for players; most rosters are built from April to August (signing windows).
  • For a player under 18 the IIHF window does not apply, but national rules do (minors).
  • Always confirm the date with the receiving club before you travel; rules change between seasons.

Frequently asked questions

When does the IIHF transfer window close for 2026/27?

On 15 February 2027 for players aged 18 and over; leagues can close earlier.

Which leagues close their registration on 31 December?

In this calendar: France's Ligue Magnus and Division 1 (31 December 2026, 23:59); the Alps Hockey League allows non-domestic changes until 30 December, 18:00.
