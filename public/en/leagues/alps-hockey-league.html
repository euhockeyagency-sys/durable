Alps Hockey League: player route and rules 2026/27

01

League position and verified status

The Alps Hockey League is a multinational senior competition involving clubs from Austria, Italy, Slovenia and Croatia. Its place in the sporting system and its administrative route depend on the country of the specific club.

02

Official 2026/27 format

Official / Verified: the league’s current League Mode describes a 13-team field and 36 regular-season games per team. The regular season runs from 19 September 2026 to 30 January 2027.

The top five advance to the Master Round with ranking-based bonus points (4–3–2–1–0) and are already qualified for the playoffs. Places 6–13 split into two Qualification Rounds with bonus points (3–2–1–0). The top three in each qualification group then contest best-of-three series for the remaining three playoff places.

Master and Qualification Rounds start on 2 February 2027; pre-playoffs on 25 February. Quarterfinals start on 6 March, semifinals on 23 March and the final on 10 April. These playoff rounds are best-of-seven; the latest possible final game is 24 April 2027. Match dates remain subject to the official schedule.

03

One league, four national settings

EHA assessment: assess the sporting role and practical package together. Clubs in Austria, Italy, Slovenia and Croatia can differ in training arrangements, resources, housing and the balance between development and senior performance.

Start with the club’s country and competent federation, then establish the exact team, training group, expected role, contract and residence or employment route. A place in the same league does not mean identical conditions or administrative requirements.

EHA can help compare realistic options and handle club communication within the agreed service scope. This guide does not confirm a current vacancy.

04

Is AlpsHL a realistic playing level?

EHA assessment: a useful profile shows repeatable senior-game contributions: skating pace, decisions under pressure, defensive detail, puck movement and a role on special teams where relevant. Statistics need context from the previous league, age, minutes and responsibilities.

  • Established senior player: show a clear role and recent evidence against comparable opposition.
  • Junior moving into senior hockey: assess physical readiness, consistency and the actual development opportunity; a place without meaningful playing time may not serve the goal.
  • Goaltender: provide full-game context and clarify the expected workload and competition for starts.

No universal points total or league label guarantees selection. For a deeper review, see EHA player assessment. For a younger player, compare the Austria junior route before assuming senior hockey is the next step.

05

Foreign-player and roster eligibility

The receiving organisation must confirm the current league rules, national-federation requirements and the player’s status for the intended competition and match date. No universal import limit is verified here for every club and player category.

An EU passport does not guarantee sporting selection. Citizenship, roster classification and lawful residence or employment are separate checks. EHA can help identify the questions that need written confirmation before a commitment.

06

Recruitment, tryouts and timing

EHA planning guidance: prepare a current profile and recent game video before the intended move. The league calendar is not an application window or proof of vacancies. Mid-season approaches depend on an actual sporting need and applicable transfer and registration deadlines.

Before accepting a trial, establish who authorised it, the dates, evaluation criteria, training group, intended role, insurance, accommodation, expenses and decision process. Paid attendance, training participation or a verbal invitation does not guarantee a contract or roster place.

Submit the profile to EHA so the level and proposed next step can be reviewed before travel.

07

AlpsHL salary, housing and full contract value

No current official league-wide salary scale is verified here. Pay and benefits must be assessed from a specific written offer; do not use an unsupported salary range as a promise.

  • Income: gross or net amount, tax responsibility, payment dates, contract duration and any conditional bonuses.
  • Accommodation: address, shared or private room, deposit, utilities, meals and local transport.
  • Hockey expenses: equipment, travel, registration, transfer charges and who pays each item.
  • Protection and exit: insurance, injury arrangements, termination, notice and travel home.

Compare guaranteed income with player-paid costs over the full contract period. EHA can help review the sporting fit and clarify the conditions of an offer. See how to compare two hockey offers.

08

Visa, residence and right to work

The law of the club’s country applies. EU/EEA and third-country citizenship may lead to different routes. An invitation, contract, registration or IIHF transfer does not itself replace lawful entry, residence or work permission.

09

IIHF transfer, federation and insurance

A move between national associations follows current IIHF transfer rules. The club must also confirm national-federation registration, AlpsHL eligibility, insurance and responsibility for fees.

10

What to send EHA for an initial review

  • birth year, position, shooting side, height, weight, current team and league;
  • citizenship and any second citizenship, country of residence and contract status;
  • verifiable statistics, an Elite Prospects or federation profile if available and recent playing role;
  • recent full-game or full-shift video with jersey colour, number and useful timestamps;
  • target season, availability and practical expectations about housing and contract conditions.

A passport scan or medical file is not needed for the initial enquiry. Prepare a hockey resume and clearly identified game video.
