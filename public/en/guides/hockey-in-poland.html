Playing hockey in Poland 2026/27: a guide for import players

A practical guide for players. League rules, level and immigration requirements must be checked for the relevant season and individual case.

Short answer: Poland’s top division is TAURON Hokej Liga, the commercial name PZHL uses; earlier regulations define it as PHL – Polska Hokej Liga, the professional men’s top division. Ten teams play in 2026/27, and PZHL says the league is closed, without relegation. Imports are common: 103 played at least five games in 2025/26. A limit of ten import players for 2026/27 has been reported, but EHA has not seen the official PZHL document, so ask the club to confirm the rule in writing.

01

League structure

For 2026/27, the official PZHL competition directory lists the current national senior and junior competitions. EHA links two main senior routes:

  • TAURON Hokej Liga — the top senior competition.
  • 1 Liga / MHL — a second-tier and development route whose age and participation rules must be checked each season.

The Polish federation also publishes junior competitions, including Junior CLJ and age-group categories. Competition names and groups can change, so use the current-season documents.

02

Comparing the levels

2026/27 verified top division: PZHL confirmed ten teams in TAURON Hokej Liga. The regular season opened on 15 September 2026, is scheduled for 45 rounds and runs to 21 February 2027, with the first play-off games planned for 26–27 February.

Level matters more than the country's name. Clubs compare pace, age, position, responsibilities, eligibility and the quality of available video.

04

Age and citizenship

For younger players, game opportunities, development potential and age eligibility matter. For senior players, clubs need a proven role, consistent production and the ability to contribute now. Citizenship affects paperwork and import status, but it does not replace sporting evaluation.

05

EU and non-EU passports

Citizenship of an EU member state generally provides easier labour-market access than third-country citizenship. It does not guarantee a roster spot or remove hockey registration requirements. Before committing to a non-EU player, the club should confirm a valid immigration and employment route.

Important: import status under sporting regulations and the legal right to work are separate questions. Both must be checked.
06

Transfer, registration, visa and work

  1. Transfer: a move between national associations uses the IIHF International Transfer Card process under the current regulations.
  2. Registration: the receiving club confirms the PZHL requirements and the rules of the specific competition.
  3. Stay: a third-country national needs a lawful immigration status that permits the intended purpose.
  4. Work: the right to work depends on citizenship, immigration status, the contract and any applicable permit or exemption.

This is general orientation, not legal advice. Before signing, verify the current-season requirements with the club and PZHL, and confirm immigration rules through Poland's official portal and the competent consulate.

Transfer timing 2026/27: the IIHF’s general window for international transfers runs from 1 July to 15 February (IIHF International Transfer Regulations in force from 6 October 2025); it does not apply to players under 18. PZHL can set an earlier registration date for its competitions; EHA has not verified the 2026/27 date, so ask the club for it in writing.

07

Living costs and recruitment timing

There is no single official hockey-wide cost-of-living figure for a player in Poland. Housing and daily costs vary by city and by what the club includes. Before accepting an offer, get written confirmation of housing cost, utilities, deposit, meals, local transport, insurance and any other player-paid expenses, then compare guaranteed income with the full monthly budget.

The main roster-building window is April to August. Targeted openings may appear later because of injuries, budget changes or roster performance. The right time to approach a club is when current statistics, a clear profile and recent video are ready.

Pay: no official salary scale exists. A third-party estimate (playhockeyineurope.com, page dated 7 September 2026) lists €15,000–60,000+ for the Polish top division, without saying gross or net. Treat it as orientation only; EHA’s own range for Poland is not published yet. See hockey player salaries in Europe.

How many imports can a Polish top-division team use in 2026/27?

A limit of ten import players has been reported for 2026/27, but EHA has not seen the official PZHL document. Ask the club to show the current rule before you commit.

Is an international transfer required?

A move between national associations normally uses the IIHF process. The club and PZHL should confirm the exact steps.

Does an EU passport guarantee a spot?

No. It affects administration, while the sporting decision remains with the club.
