Leagues · 2026/27

Most open leagues
for import players

The European competitions where an import is most likely to find a realistic vacancy — mostly second and third tiers, with the top divisions kept separate because their thresholds are higher.

EHA assessment

Openness here is an EHA assessment, not a guarantee. It reflects how often clubs at each level sign imports and how realistic a first spot tends to be — drawn from our Hockey Market Map 2026. A league being “more open” does not mean a spot is easy or that quotas do not apply. Always open the league page and check the current season’s roster rules, import limits and work-eligibility before acting.

Third tier and entry levels

The most accessible starting points. Rosters turn over more, budgets are smaller, and a clear, honest profile with video is enough to open a conversation.

  • Germany — Oberliga — many clubs across two regional groups; openings appear here most often.
  • Finland — Suomi-sarja — a realistic entry point into the Finnish structure.
  • Sweden — HockeyEttan — regional third tier; an EU passport helps a lot.
  • Poland — 1 Liga / MHL — a development-focused route into a growing market.

Second tier, realistic with the right profile

Professional second divisions that regularly carry imports. The bar is higher than the entry levels, but a specific role and verifiable video make a case.

  • Slovakia — TIPOS SHL — the Slovak second level is fairly open to suitable profiles.
  • Norway — Hockeyliga1 — often more accessible than the top league.
  • Denmark — 1. division — a smaller market, but a defined role can be enough.
  • Netherlands — Eredivisie and CEHL — national and cross-border senior routes.
  • France — Division 1 — a second senior level where roster need and eligibility must be checked.

How to use this list

Pick two or three leagues where your profile fits the role a club actually needs, not the most familiar name. Then read each league page for the season format, foreign-player rules and the application package, and confirm your passport and work status. If you want a realistic read on where you fit, send your profile — assessment first, then a targeted approach to clubs.
