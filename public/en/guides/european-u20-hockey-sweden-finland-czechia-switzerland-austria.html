European U20 hockey: Sweden, Finland, Czechia, Switzerland and Austria

Top junior levels in five countries compared by age rules, foreign-player rules, costs, housing, school and the step into senior hockey.

Five countries · Costs and school · Into senior hockey · Next step

Short answer

Sweden's U20 Nationell, Finland's U20 SM-sarja, Czechia's Extraliga juniorů, Switzerland's U21-Elit and Austria's cross-border U20 International League are each country's top junior level in 2026/27, but they differ in age rules and in how they treat foreign players. Czechia allows at most two foreign-national juniors per game and none in goal; Switzerland's top level is U21, not U20; Sweden and Finland set special checks for international minors. Family costs, school and housing are agreed club by club — there is no official price list.

Choose by the player's age in the target season, eligibility as a foreign player, school and the realistic step into senior hockey, in that order.

Costs, housing and school

No federation publishes a family price list, and EHA has no reliable sample of junior costs, so we quote no average. Ask each club in writing for: housing (host family, flat or boarding), meals, school fees and language support, transport, equipment, insurance, and who pays flights and the transfer. Compare total season cost, not the headline fee. More: junior season cost calculator and school and education abroad.

  • School: admission is separate from team selection; a “hockey programme” label does not guarantee a place or English teaching.
  • Housing for minors: name the responsible adult, emergency contact and parental consents (child safety and host families).
  • Residence: non-EU minors need a residence basis such as study; a tryout invitation is not one.

The step into senior hockey

Each system links juniors to senior hockey differently: loans and two-team arrangements in Finland and Sweden, development licences in some countries, and the Alps Hockey League's age points in the U20i region. Ask the club which senior team its U20 players have practised and played with in recent seasons. See after U20.

Frequently asked questions

Which European U20 league is the strongest?

Each country's top level (U20 Nationell, U20 SM-sarja, Extraliga juniorů, U21-Elit, U20i) is strong in its own system; the right choice depends on role, eligibility and school.

Can a foreign junior play in the Czech Extraliga juniorů?

Yes, within the rule: at most two players of the defined foreign-national category per game, and not as goalies.

Does Switzerland have a U20 league?

Its top junior level is U21-Elit; age is counted as 2027 minus birth year in 2026/27.

How much does a season in a European U20 team cost?

There is no official price list. Ask each club for housing, school, meals, transport, insurance and equipment in writing.
