Hockey in Austria: junior pathways, leagues and costs

For players and parents comparing a move to Austria. Start with the right playing level, then check education, accommodation, the full budget and eligibility. EHA helps assess the profile and plan the next steps.

Discuss your Austria route with EHA
01

League structure

For 2026/27, the official league and federation sources confirm these principal senior routes:

  • ICE Hockey League — top multinational professional level; the official 2026/27 format has 14 teams and 52 regular-season games per team
  • Alps Hockey League — multinational senior level with the 2026/27 competition active across participating federations

ÖEHV also publishes national competitions, including the Ö Eishockey Liga and current U20/U18 competitions. The ICE Hockey League regular season began on 18 September 2026. Check the current Game Center and season regulations for exact participants, age eligibility and registration rules.

Which Austrian hockey route fits your profile?

EHA assessment: these are selection considerations, not official entry rules or confirmed vacancies.

  • ICE Hockey League: a demanding professional target. The profile needs evidence of a useful role against comparable senior opposition; a strong highlight reel alone does not establish that fit.
  • Alps Hockey League: assess senior readiness, expected role and development context individually. The league name does not establish an import opening, salary or housing package.
  • Junior hockey: compare the age group, coaching, competition for minutes and school arrangements before choosing a programme. A junior place does not guarantee promotion to a senior team.

Send EHA the current level, age, position and recent game video so we can assess a realistic starting point.

04

Junior hockey: what parents need to check

Official / Verified, season 2026/27: ÖEHV lists U20 International League, U18 International League and national U16 competition. Age-group names alone do not establish a player’s eligibility: the applicable season regulations and registration approval must be checked.

EHA assessment: a suitable programme needs a workable life outside the rink as well as a sporting fit. Before committing, establish:

  • the school, admission requirements, teaching language and how lessons fit with practices and away games;
  • the accommodation, responsible adult, daily supervision, meals and travel between school and rink;
  • the exact team, training group, selection process and how playing opportunities are earned;
  • the full season cost, payment dates, injury arrangements and cancellation or refund terms;
  • who handles registration, transfer and the minor’s required permissions.

Continue with our Austria junior hockey guide and guide for hockey parents. EHA can help the family compare the sporting route and the questions that need written answers.

05

EU and non-EU players: separate the checks

Citizenship, residence status and sporting eligibility are separate questions. An EU passport does not guarantee selection or establish a player’s status under league roster rules. For a non-EU player, the planned stay and any paid activity need an appropriate legal route.

The official Austrian portal lists a residence category for eligible pupils at qualifying educational institutions. A hockey programme invitation alone does not prove that the pupil route applies. For a minor, the family must also establish the arrangements for care and any accompanying parent’s own right to stay.

See our EU and non-EU hockey guide and minor-player documents and transfer checklist.

06

Transfer, registration, visa and work

  1. Transfer: an international move normally follows the current IIHF process.
  2. Registration: the receiving club confirms federation and league requirements.
  3. Residence: the player needs a lawful basis for the intended stay.
  4. Work: the contract, permit and immigration status must allow the activity.

The official Migration Austria portal lists professional athletes within the Other Key Workers route. For 2026 it gives professional athletes 20 bonus points and states a statutory minimum of €3,465 gross per month for that route, together with a labour-market test and a 55-point minimum. This is an immigration threshold for one residence/work route, not a statement of hockey market salary and not an automatic permit for every contract.

General orientation only, not legal advice. Check the current season with the club and federation, and immigration requirements with the official authority or competent consulate.

07

Austria hockey costs and recruitment planning

No single price covers every Austrian hockey route. Compare the same full-season items for each option; do not treat a programme fee as the family’s total budget.

  • Hockey: programme or club fees, camps, trials, equipment and any separately charged games or travel.
  • School and daily life: tuition if applicable, language support, housing, deposit, meals, utilities and local transport.
  • Administration and contingency: insurance, transfer and registration charges, residence applications, travel home and an emergency reserve.
  • Contract terms: what is included, who pays each item, payment dates and what happens after injury, non-selection or an early departure.

We do not publish a standard academy price, salary or benefits package without a current, attributable source. Ask EHA to help compare the written conditions of a specific option.

EHA planning guidance: prepare the profile before the next season’s selection process. Spring and summer are useful planning periods for many moves; they are not an official Austrian application window. Mid-season opportunities depend on actual needs and applicable registration or transfer deadlines. A paid trial is not a roster guarantee.

What to send EHA for an initial review

  • name, birth year, position, height, shooting side and current team and league;
  • an Elite Prospects link if available, plus verifiable statistics and recent playing role;
  • recent full-game or full-shift video, with jersey colour and number and useful timestamps;
  • citizenship, current country of residence, contract status and intended move date;
  • for juniors: school year, language level, accommodation needs and the family’s approximate season budget.

A passport scan or medical file is not needed for the initial enquiry. Prepare a hockey resume and clear game video. For a deeper sporting review, see EHA player assessment.

Is an international transfer normally required?

A move between national associations normally follows the applicable IIHF process. The receiving club and federation must confirm the steps and any minor-player requirements.

Does an EU passport guarantee a place?

No. Sporting selection, league roster eligibility and legal residence or work status are separate checks.

When should a player apply?

Prepare before selection for the intended season. EHA uses spring and summer as planning guidance for many moves, not an official application window. Actual opportunities and registration deadlines must be checked individually.

Can a junior combine hockey and school in Austria?

This depends on the particular programme, school admission, timetable, accommodation and lawful residence route. A hockey invitation does not by itself confirm school admission or a residence permit.

How much does a season in Austria cost?

There is no single verified total for every route. Compare written hockey, school, housing, food, travel, insurance and administrative costs for the full season.
