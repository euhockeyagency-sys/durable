ICEHL and Alps Hockey League salaries: what players earn in 2026/27

Approximate ranges per season, what drives the number, and what a contract includes beyond salary.

Prepared by the European Hockey Agency editorial team. Updated 16 November 2026.

Short answer: what players earn

By EHA’s approximate estimate, an ICEHL player earns roughly €35,000 to €150,000 or more per season, and an Alps Hockey League player earns €15,000 to €45,000. Neither league publishes an official salary scale, so the individual contract decides the number.

Approximate salary ranges per season, EHA estimate
LeagueLevelApproximate range per season
ICEHLTop tier, 14 clubs from Austria, Italy, Slovenia and Hungary€35,000 – €150,000+
Alps Hockey LeagueSecond tier, 13 clubs from Austria, Italy, Slovenia and Croatia€15,000 – €45,000

These are approximate market reference points, not figures from specific contracts. Clubs quote salaries in different ways, so always ask whether a figure is gross or net.

Why the range is so wide

Both leagues are international, and club budgets differ a lot. The low end is a young or depth player at a small-budget club. The high end is a top-line import at a club with a large budget.

The number depends on:

  • Role. Top-pair defensemen, first-line forwards and starting goalies are paid clearly more.
  • Roster status. A player who takes an import spot has to make the team better and is paid accordingly.
  • Previous league and stats. Clubs weigh the level where the points were scored, not the point total.
  • Club country. One league spans four countries with different taxes and living costs.
  • Signing date. A mid-season replacement signs on different terms than a summer signing.

What a contract includes beyond salary

Two offers cannot be compared on salary alone. Ask the club to state in writing:

  • apartment and utilities;
  • car or transport;
  • health insurance and pay during injury;
  • flights at the start and end of the season;
  • equipment and sticks;
  • playoff and performance bonuses;
  • the number of months paid.

In the Alps Hockey League, housing and meals can be a large part of the package. A €20,000 offer with an apartment and insurance can be worth more than €25,000 without them. The guide “Two team offers” shows how to set two packages side by side.

ICEHL or Alps Hockey League

The 2026/27 ICEHL regular season is 52 games per team. The Alps Hockey League regular season is 36 games. For a player aged 19–22, a second-tier league with real ice time is often more useful than a fourth-line spot in the ICEHL. The Alps Hockey League is not an automatic step up to the ICEHL.

What to check before signing

  1. Whether the figure is gross or net.
  2. How many months the club pays and on which dates.
  3. What happens to payments after an injury or termination.
  4. Who pays for housing, insurance and the work permit.
  5. Your roster status on the date of the first game.

The full list is in the guide “How to verify a hockey club offer”.

How this compares with third-party estimates

The third-party site playhockeyineurope.com (page dated 7 September 2026) lists €40,000 – €150,000+ for the ICEHL and €18,000 – €45,000 for the Alps Hockey League. EHA’s estimate is close to these figures, with a slightly lower floor. Both are approximate. Other leagues are covered in “Hockey player salaries in Europe”.
