    From North America

    How your history maps to European levels

    • Regular ECHL minutes, or a strong NCAA Division I / U Sports career with a defined role: researching second divisions such as DEL2 or the Alps Hockey League, and top divisions of smaller countries, is a reasonable start.
    • Junior A (USHL, NAHL, BCHL) or NCAA Division III: third levels and some second divisions are the usual first look; a role and video matter more than points.
    • AHL experience: top divisions such as Ligue Magnus or the ICE Hockey League can be realistic, depending on role.

    This is EHA's planning guide, not a rule: European clubs judge age, role, recent minutes and full-game video together with the league name. Import limits differ by league — see the league pages.

    How the hockey level calculator works

    This hockey level calculator is a short, rule-based model. It does not watch video or compare you with other players. Here is exactly what it does with your answers.

    What you enter

    • birth year, position (forward, defence or goaltender) and whether you hold an EU passport;
    • the category of the league you play in now, from a top division of a strong hockey country down to amateur or college level;
    • points and games played last season (not required for goaltenders) and the number of professional seasons;
    • your goal: a professional contract, junior development or playing while studying.

    How the result is formed

    1. Current league category gives the starting score and carries the most weight.
    2. Production: for skaters with at least 10 games, points per game are compared with position benchmarks (higher for forwards than for defencemen) and move the score up or down. Goaltenders are assessed on level and experience only.
    3. Age: younger players get a small bonus for potential; the adjustment turns negative after 27 and more so after 31.
    4. Professional seasons: three or more add a little; none subtracts a little. A top-league category with no professional seasons is capped, because that combination is not a coherent signal.
    5. The score is turned into one of three bands: top and second divisions, second and third levels, or third level.
    6. Leagues from the EHA database are then filtered by that band. Without an EU passport, leagues tagged as hard to access for imports are left out. Junior leagues are shown only to players aged 19 or younger. Up to eight leagues are listed.

    Your goal changes the advice shown under the result, not the score.

    What it does not consider

    The calculator does not check your exact citizenship or a second passport, national rules such as France's locally trained player (JFL) status or Czechia's foreign-player limit, registration and transfer deadlines, visa and work-permit thresholds, skating, size, injuries or the strength of your particular team. It also cannot verify the statistics you type in.

    Why the result is preliminary

    Two players with the same numbers can be very different: one plays first-unit power play, the other third-line minutes. Clubs decide on role, video and references, and on whether you fit their import rules. That is why the result is a shortlist to research, not a verdict, and why EHA asks for full-game video, verifiable statistics and a description of your role before giving an assessment.

    From result to country and to EHA

    Open the country guides linked in your result, for example Sweden, Germany or Czechia, to check levels, rules and costs. If you arrive from a country guide, the result also tells you which of that country's leagues fall within your range. Compare divisions in the European league comparison, prepare your resume and highlight video, then send your profile for an initial review.

    Calculator questions

    How should I use the calculator result?

    Use it as an initial shortlist for research. The model uses your current league category, scoring relative to position, age and seasons played. It does not assess video, verify your statistics or measure the strength of every league.

    Does the calculator guarantee a contract?

    No. The result does not confirm vacancies, interest from clubs or eligibility. A personal review of your video and statistics, followed by checks with the relevant club and federation, is needed.

    Does an EU passport remove import limits?

    Not automatically. Import classification depends on the competition rules and the player's individual status. Permission to work, registration and international transfer requirements are separate checks. The calculator's passport filter is a broad model assumption, not an eligibility decision.
