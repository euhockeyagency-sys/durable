Andersen Liga: player route and rules 2026/27

A verified guide to Hungary’s Andersen Liga: competition status, roster rules, recruitment, contracts, immigration and transfers.

01

League position and verified status

Andersen Liga is a Hungarian senior competition administered under MJSZ rules. Treat it as a distinct national route below the cross-border Erste Liga and verify each club’s current sporting and administrative needs.

02

Confirmed 2026/27 framework

The published rules set a minimum age of 16 and a transfer deadline of 15 December 2026. The final team field is not fixed here because the application deadline is 15 August 2026.

03

Roster and foreign-player rules

The published maximum is three foreign players in a match. The club and MJSZ must confirm how a player is classified, registered and eligible; a vacancy and legal right to work are separate questions.

04

Clubs and choosing a target

Target clubs by position, role, level, roster construction and an authorised sporting contact. Participation in the competition does not prove a vacancy. Confirm the club, contact and exact need before sharing documents or paying costs.

05

What clubs assess

Clubs compare skating pace, decisions under pressure, role, special-teams value, verified recent statistics, full-game video, health, contract status, citizenship and administrative readiness. The application must show value for this specific level.

06

Recruitment timing and tryouts

A trial, invitation or verbal discussion is not a contract. Confirm who invited the player, dates, evaluation criteria, travel, insurance, accommodation, equipment, expenses and the deadline for a written decision.

07

Contract, pay and housing

There is no official universal salary table for every club. Put gross or net pay, taxes, payment dates, bonuses, housing, deposit, utilities, meals, travel, equipment, insurance, injury protection, termination and return travel in writing.

08

Residence and right to work

Immigration and employment rules depend on citizenship and the country where the club is based. A league registration or invitation does not grant entry, residence or work permission; verify the competent national authority before travel or employment.

09

IIHF transfer, licence and insurance

An international move follows current IIHF transfer rules, but an IIHF transfer does not replace federation registration, competition eligibility, insurance, immigration permission or a valid contract. Confirm responsibility and fees for every step.

10

Player application package

Send passport and any second citizenship; birth year, position, shot, height and weight; current team; verified statistics; Elite Prospects or federation profile; role-based highlights and a recent full game; contract or release status; health and availability.
