What the club receives

Relevant profiles, not a mass list

  • Profiles matched to the position, role and team level
  • Elite Prospects, current statistics and available video where applicable
  • Citizenship, contract status and known availability
  • A direct route for sporting and practical follow-up questions

A useful request includes

Details that improve the match

  • League, age group and the role inside your system
  • Position, shooting side, player type and priority skills
  • Import, passport or registration constraints that must be checked
  • Timing, trial format and the relevant compensation or budget framework

More precise criteria help reduce unsuitable introductions. EHA does not promise that every request will produce a candidate.

Sporting fit

What we evaluate

  • Level of previous competition and current role
  • Position-specific needs, production and available video
  • Age, development route and expected use by the coaching staff
  • Timing, contract status and willingness to consider the market

Eligibility is separate

What still requires official verification

  • Federation registration and international transfer requirements
  • Import or passport rules for the relevant league and season
  • Entry, residence and work authorization where applicable
  • Final contract, medical and club approval

A sporting match is not an official eligibility decision. The relevant federation and public authorities determine registration, immigration and work authorization.

FAQ for clubs

Questions before sending a player request

What information should a club include in a player request?

Include the league and team level, age group, position, role, shooting side, passport or import restrictions, timing, trial format and the relevant compensation or budget framework.

Does submitting a request guarantee a suitable player?

No. EHA reviews the request and responds when a reasonably suitable and available profile can be identified. Availability and any final agreement depend on the player, club and applicable rules.

What information can accompany a player profile?

Where available and appropriate, the profile can include an Elite Prospects link, current statistics, video, position, shooting side, citizenship, contract status and availability.

Can EHA help with passport, transfer and visa questions?

EHA can help identify questions that require verification and coordinate the sporting process. The club, federation and competent authorities remain responsible for official eligibility, registration, immigration and employment decisions.

What happens after the club sends a request?

EHA reviews the sporting criteria, clarifies missing details, checks relevant profiles and contacts the club if a reasonable match is available for discussion.
