# Country pages: fact-check notes

## Poland: right to work for non-EU players (checked 21 November 2026)

Used in the `#visa-work` section of `guides/hockey-in-poland` and `ru/guides/hokkej-v-polshe`.

- **DIRECT (search copy of official text)**, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1620, regulation of the Minister of Family, Labour and Social Policy of 20 November 2025 (gov.pl attachment e6c2117b…): a work permit is not required for an athlete working for a sports club based in Poland for up to 30 days in a calendar year. In force from 1 December 2025 (Zielona Linia, zielonalinia.gov.pl).
- **DIRECT**, Zielona Linia (zielonalinia.gov.pl, government labour-market helpline), «Powierzenie pracy cudzoziemcowi na podstawie oświadczenia»: the declaration applies to citizens of Armenia, Belarus, Moldova and Ukraine; the work period may not exceed 24 months; the Polish employing entity files it via praca.gov.pl. «Ważne zmiany … od 1 grudnia 2025»: from 1 December 2025 only these four countries; Georgia removed.
- **DIRECT**, Zielona Linia, «Cudzoziemcy zwolnieni z zezwolenia na pracę na mocy ustawy» (stan prawny 1.09.2025): under art. 3(5) of the Act of 20 March 2025, a foreigner staying legally may work without a permit or declaration if he holds a valid Karta Polaka.
- **DIRECT**, Zielona Linia, «Zezwolenie na pracę – informacje ogólne» (stan prawny 8.09.2026): the employer files the work-permit application via praca.gov.pl; applications filed any other way are not considered.
- Ukrainians with temporary protection: separate notification procedure (Powiatowy Urząd Pracy, March 2026, SEARCH) — stated on the page only as "a separate procedure exists".
- Not stated on the page: permit fees, processing times, salary thresholds. Russia and Kazakhstan are named only as examples of countries outside the declaration list.

## Slovakia: right to work for non-EU players (checked 21 November 2026)

Used in `#visa-work` of `guides/hockey-in-slovakia` and `ru/guides/hokkej-v-slovakii`.

- **DIRECT**, Act 5/2004 on employment services, consolidated version effective 1 August 2026 (static.slov-lex.sk): the § 23a list of cases without a work permit / vacancy confirmation contains no item for athletes or sports clubs (closest item: performing artists at an artistic event).
- **DIRECT**, Slovak Interior Ministry, foreign police FAQ no. 16 (July 2025): documents for temporary residence for employment under § 23 of the Aliens Act — travel document; proof of purpose by the ÚPSVaR confirmation that the vacancy can be filled (§ 23(1)) or by a work permit / employer confirmation that no permit is needed (§ 23(2)); criminal record not older than 90 days (home state and states of stay over 90 days in the last three years); proof of accommodation not older than 90 days; medical certificate within 30 days of receiving the residence document; administrative fee.
- **DIRECT but dated (2009)**, Ministry of Labour / IOM brochure «Zamestnávanie cudzincov na Slovensku»: EU/EEA citizens need no work permit; a work permit alone does not allow work to start — temporary residence for employment is required. Used only for these two general statements.
- Not stated on the page: residence permit duration, the 90-day exception and fees (only found in search copies of 404/2011; re-check on slov-lex before adding).
