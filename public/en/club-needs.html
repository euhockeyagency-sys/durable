Club needs
confirmed requests from clubs

Positions that hockey clubs have confirmed to EHA. Each request shows only what the club confirmed: country, league, season, position, age, citizenship or import status and known conditions. When nothing is open, the list says so.

Current club requests

How a request gets onto this page

  1. The club confirms a need. A club tells EHA about a specific position for a specific season and level.
  2. EHA checks the details. League, season, position, age range, citizenship or import status and conditions are recorded only as the club confirmed them. Anything the club did not confirm is shown as “not confirmed by the club”.
  3. Publication with the club’s agreement. A request appears only when the club agrees that it can be public. Club contact details are never published.
  4. Reconfirmation and closing. EHA reconfirms open requests with the club. A request is removed when the place is filled, when its validity ends or when the club does not reconfirm it.

What a club request is not

  • It is not a contract, a roster place or a visa. The club still assesses each player, and an offer exists only in writing.
  • A field marked “not confirmed by the club” means the information is unknown, not that it is favourable.
  • Citizenship and import status for sport, registration and the right to work are separate checks. See the visas and transfers guide.
  • Be careful with anyone who asks for money for a “guaranteed place”: see red flags in hockey offers.

What to send for a request

Name the request (its code is shown on the card) and send: birth year, position and shooting side, height and weight, citizenship, current team and league, verifiable statistics, a recent full-game video and the date you are available. Templates: hockey resume for European clubs and video for clubs.
