# Switzerland: fact-check log
## 2026-11-15 review: National League foreign-player (import) rules

Pages: `leagues/switzerland-national-league` (EN/RU), country guides `hockey-in-switzerland` / `hokkej-v-shvejcarii`.

Primary sources read on 2026-11-15:
- National League, Weisungen Spielbetrieb 2025/26, section 1.3 (https://www.nationalleague.ch/media/pkqbsytq/weisungen_spielbetrieb_nl_25_26_d.pdf): quota 10 foreign players per club; 6 usable per game in the NL (4 in the NL/SL qualification); dual citizens and "Wie Schweizer" players do not count, even with an international transfer card; a quota place is used when the foreign player is on the game sheet at the start of a league game; club-change break for foreign players from 15 Feb 2026, 24:00 (IIHF online approval also required by then).
- Clubs' 2021 decision (League release, copy on evz.ch): from 2022/23 foreign players per team and game: 4 with 12 clubs, 5 with 13, 6 with 14; a reduction takes effect two seasons later; from 2026/27 a foreign player trained in Switzerland keeps "Wie Schweizer" only until the 23rd year of age, then counts against the quota unless he holds a Swiss passport.

Not confirmed:
- The 2026/27 Weisungen Spielbetrieb (listed at https://www.nationalleague.ch/page/reglemente) was not opened; 6/10 for 2026/27 is inferred from the 2025/26 directive plus the 2021 rule and SIHF's 14-club list. Pages state this.
- Age cap: league-linked sources say 23; SRF reported 22. Pages use 23 (primary 2021 text) and flag the SRF discrepancy.
- 2026/27 transfer deadline date.

Next check: when the 2026/27 directive is readable, confirm quota, per-game number, age cap and deadline, then drop the caveats.
