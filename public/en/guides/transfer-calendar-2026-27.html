Hockey transfer calendar 2026/27: international transfers and league deadlines

Every country in the directory, filterable by country, league and category, with the official source and check date for each row.

Updated 18 November 2026.

Short answer

For season 2026/27 the IIHF window for international transfers of adult players runs from 1 July 2026 to 15 February 2027, but each league sets its own registration deadline inside it — for example 30 December 2026 for non-domestic changes in the Alps Hockey League, 31 December 2026 in France, 11 January 2027 in Italy's second level and 15 February 2027 in DEL2, Poland's top league and Switzerland's National League (foreign players). How to use the calendar

  • A deadline is the last day for paperwork, not the time clubs look for players; most rosters are built from April to August (signing windows).
  • For a player under 18 the IIHF window does not apply, but national rules do (minors).
  • Always confirm the date with the receiving club before you travel; rules change between seasons.

Frequently asked questions

When does the IIHF transfer window close for 2026/27?

On 15 February 2027 for players aged 18 and over; leagues can close earlier.

Which leagues close their registration on 31 December?

In this calendar: France's Ligue Magnus and Division 1 (31 December 2026, 23:59); the Alps Hockey League allows non-domestic changes until 30 December, 18:00.
