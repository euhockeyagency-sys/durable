Ice hockey salaries in Europe: pay by league and contract terms

There is no single European ice hockey salary. A useful comparison needs the league, player's role, currency, gross or net basis, paid months and housing terms. A published minimum, a previous year's taxable income and an agency estimate answer different questions.

This guide covers ice hockey. The evidence below spans different years; it is not a verified salary scale for every league in 2026/27.

Quick answer: unofficial third-party ranges in euros, as listed by playhockeyineurope.com (7 September 2026; gross or net and season length not stated): SHL 80,000–300,000+, Liiga 75,000–300,000, DEL 90,000–350,000, Czech Extraliga 50,000–250,000+, ICEHL 40,000–150,000+, DEL2 25,000–90,000, HockeyAllsvenskan 18,000–48,000, Alps Hockey League 18,000–45,000, Polish league 15,000–60,000+, Oberliga imports 15,000–40,000. They are orientation, not league scales: see the full table and limits, and the separate ICEHL and Alps Hockey League salary guide.

What the sources actually establish

SHL: historical minimums and taxable income

SICO's current player page links collective agreements for SHL and HockeyAllsvenskan. The linked SHL PDF, updated 11 May 2020, records monthly rookie amounts of SEK 22,000 at age 18 to SEK 25,000 at age 21, effective from 1 May 2019. It also provides for annual adjustments. These are historical amounts, not a verified 2026/27 minimum.

The third-party site hockeyarena.se, updated 25 September 2026, reports an average of SEK 842,960 for 2025 taxable income, excluding players without recorded income. That is the site's calculation from a prior tax year, not an official current league salary average or take-home pay.

HockeyAllsvenskan: individual pay terms

Primary document. The HockeyAllsvenskan agreement linked by SICO, updated 6 May 2020, states in §4 that employer and player agree pay and other compensation. The document also contains joint negotiating material explicitly marked SHL-only: those SHL minimums must not be applied to HockeyAllsvenskan. This source does not verify a 2026/27 average. See the HockeyAllsvenskan guide.

Hockeyettan: a club example, not a salary scale

Official league publication, 2026/27. In a Hockeyettan interview published 28 October 2026, Örnsköldsviks HF’s sporting director reports no fixed monthly player pay. He describes compensation for lost work income on weekday away trips. This establishes one club’s reported arrangement, not a zero salary for every Hockeyettan player. See the Hockeyettan guide.

Liiga

Yle's report of 21 January 2026 describes rising offers for specific players. It reports a net contract below €200,000 for Patrick Curry and an employer cost around €400,000 after taxes and charges. This illustrates why net pay and a club's total cost must not be compared as if they were the same salary. It is reporting, not a league-wide pay table.

Liiga salary: what the 2026–27 collective agreement says. The agreement between SM-liiga Oy and the Finnish Players' Association, dated 15 June 2026 and valid for the 2026–27 season, does not publish a minimum salary or a pay scale; pay sits in each Player Contract and its fee appendix, which is not part of the public text. It does fix pay-related terms: injury loss-of-earnings compensation of 85% of the season salary, calculated on a maximum of €143,620 for 2026 (§3.4.2); the contract salary is the total including holiday pay, with holiday pay at 9% of monthly salary (11.5% after one year) (§3.5.6); a 4.5% pension premium on salary (§3.4.3); and pay through the last game if the club plays into May (§4). The copy we checked has an unsigned signature block, so confirm the final text with the league or the players' association.

ICEHL, DEL2 and AlpsHL: compare the written package

A current league-wide salary scale for these competitions was not established in this review. This does not prove that no applicable minimum or contractual rule exists. Ask for the currency, gross or net basis, payment period, housing, bonuses and injury or termination terms in writing. Do not annualise a monthly amount without knowing the paid months.

Use the ICEHL, DEL2 and Alps Hockey League guides to assess sporting fit separately from pay. To see which division band your profile points to first, try the level calculator. EHA’s approximate ranges for two of these leagues are in ICEHL and Alps Hockey League salaries.

How to compare two club offers

EHA practical checklist. Compare the same paid period and only the guaranteed terms of a written offer.

  1. Cash: currency, gross or net pay, paid months, payment dates, deductions and tax responsibility.
  2. Housing: who pays rent, deposit and utilities; a private room or shared accommodation.
  3. Other costs: food, transport, flights, equipment, insurance, registration and transfer.
  4. Risks: injury pay, trial conditions, termination and return travel. Keep conditional bonuses separate.

Cash remaining over the paid period = guaranteed cash after tax − player-paid costs over that same period. Show club-paid housing as a separate benefit; do not add its value to cash in hand. Do not budget for outside employment until the right to do that work is confirmed.

Parents: a junior pathway has a different budget

Do not use a senior professional's salary as the budget for a junior placement. First establish whether the offer is a paid contract, expense support or a programme with family-paid fees. School, housing, meals, insurance and travel need their own written breakdown.

NHL, AHL and ECHL salaries: a separate comparison

The NHLPA FAQ lists the NHL minimum at US$850,000 for 2026/27. This is an NHL minimum, not a European salary benchmark. Older AHL and ECHL minimums previously quoted on this page have been removed to avoid presenting an expired season's figures as current. Use the applicable league agreement and season; a team payroll cap is not one player's wage.

Salary questions from players and parents

How much do ICEHL players make?

There is no official ICEHL salary scale. EHA’s approximate estimate is €35,000 to €150,000 or more per season; a third-party site lists €40,000 to €150,000+. Neither says gross or net. See ICEHL and Alps Hockey League salaries for the range and what to check in an offer.

What is the average HockeyAllsvenskan salary?

A current official average is not verified in this guide. The SICO-linked agreement dated 6 May 2020 says pay and other compensation are agreed by employer and player; it does not establish a 2026/27 average.

Are European hockey salaries gross or net?

There is no universal convention. Ask for the currency, gross or net basis, tax responsibility, paid months, deductions and payment dates in writing.

Does a hockey salary include housing?

Not automatically. Confirm rent, deposits, utilities, room sharing and whether the housing arrangement ends if the player is released.

Do junior players receive a salary?

Establish the exact offer: a paid contract, expense support and a family-funded programme are different arrangements. Do not apply senior salary figures to a junior season.

Do Hockeyettan players receive a salary?

Do not assume one arrangement across the league. A Hockeyettan interview of 28 October 2026 reports no fixed monthly pay at Örnsköldsviks HF, with compensation for lost work income on weekday away trips. This is one club’s example; check the actual written offer.

Is a DEL2 salary figure monthly or per season?

Check the stated period before comparing. A monthly sum does not establish annual income: confirm the number of paid months, gross or net basis, housing and conditional bonuses. No current league-wide DEL2 salary scale is verified in this guide.
