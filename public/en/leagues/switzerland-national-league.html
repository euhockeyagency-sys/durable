Swiss National League: import rules and player route 2026/27

A verified player guide to Switzerland’s top professional league: import rules (the foreign-player quota), recruitment, eligibility, contracts, immigration and transfers.

Compare the National League to Czechia's Tipsport Extraliga →

01

League position and verified status

National League is Switzerland’s highest professional men’s ice hockey competition. SIHF identifies it as the top-level league, while National League AG organises the competition under national and international regulations.

02

2026/27 competition framework

The current SIHF Game Center lists 14 National League clubs for 2026/27. Use the live calendar, standings and league directives for exact dates, competition stages and any season-specific roster rules.

03

Clubs and choosing a target

Target a club by position, role, roster construction, recent playing level, language environment and authorised sporting contact. A club’s presence in the National League does not prove that it has a vacancy or interest in a particular player.

04

What clubs assess

At this level clubs assess elite skating, decisions under pressure, role value, special teams, verified recent statistics, full-game video, health, contract status, citizenship and administrative readiness. The package must show a clear competitive upgrade.

05

Import rules: foreign-player quota and “like a Swiss” status

The National League limits players with foreign citizenship in two ways. The official League directive for 2025/26 sets a quota of 10 foreign players per club and allows 6 to be used in a game. The clubs decided in 2021 that the number per team and game depends on league size: 4 with 12 clubs, 5 with 13 and 6 with 14. SIHF lists 14 clubs for 2026/27, so the same limit is expected to apply. A cut in the number takes effect only from the season after next.

  • Who does not count: dual citizens and players with the “like a Swiss” (Wie Schweizer) status do not use a quota place, even if they need an international transfer card.
  • When a place is used: a quota place counts as used when a foreign player is on the game report at the start of a league game.
  • “Like a Swiss” status: the clubs’ 2021 decision limits it from 2026/27: a foreign player trained in Switzerland who earns it in Swiss youth hockey keeps it in the National League only until age 23, and then counts against the quota unless he has a Swiss passport. One Swiss broadcaster reported the cut-off as 22, so the exact age must be confirmed.
  • Transfer deadline: in 2025/26 club changes of foreign players closed on 15 February 2026 at 24:00. The 2026/27 date must be checked.

The League lists a 2026/27 game-operations directive on its regulations page, but it could not be opened on 15 November 2026. The limits above are confirmed for 2025/26 and are carried forward from the 2021 clubs’ decision, not read from the 2026/27 text. The club must confirm the current directive, the player’s classification and licence history, and any game-sheet restriction in writing before signing.

06

Recruitment timing and tryouts

A trial, invitation or verbal discussion is not a contract. Confirm the inviting decision-maker, dates, evaluation criteria, travel, insurance, accommodation, equipment, costs and the deadline for a written decision before travelling.

07

Contract, pay and housing

There is no official universal salary table covering every club and role. Put gross or net pay, taxes, bonuses, payment dates, housing, deposit, utilities, transport, equipment, insurance, injury protection, termination and return travel in writing.

08

Residence and right to work

Switzerland uses different routes for EU/EFTA and non-EU/EFTA nationals. SEM states that EU/EFTA permits are handled by the cantons under the free-movement framework. For non-EU/EFTA nationals, admission is more restrictive: the employer must meet labour-market requirements and employment conditions must be customary for the region, profession and sector. A hockey contract or licence does not itself create the right to work.

09

IIHF transfer, licence and insurance

An international move follows current IIHF transfer rules, but an IIHF transfer does not replace SIHF registration, competition eligibility, insurance, immigration permission or a valid contract. Confirm responsibility and fees for every step.

10

Player application package

Send passport and any second citizenship; birth year, position, shot, height and weight; current team; verified statistics; Elite Prospects or federation profile; role-based highlights and a recent full game; contract or release status; health and availability.

What are the import rules in the Swiss National League?

Under the League’s 2025/26 directive a club has a quota of 10 foreign players and may use 6 in a game. The clubs decided that 6 applies with 14 clubs, and SIHF lists 14 for 2026/27. Dual citizens and “like a Swiss” players do not count, and the club must confirm the current rule.

What level is the National League?

It is Switzerland’s highest professional men’s ice hockey competition, with 14 clubs listed by SIHF for 2026/27.

Does a roster place guarantee eligibility?

No. The club and the competent bodies must confirm licence, roster, transfer and immigration status.

Is there one official salary table for every club?

No. Contract terms differ by club and role and must be confirmed in writing.

Does an IIHF transfer grant the right to work?

No. Transfer, registration and immigration or employment permission are separate processes.
