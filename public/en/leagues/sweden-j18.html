Sweden U18 Nationell (J18): player route and checks

A practical, cautious overview of the Swedish U18 route for a player and family. Confirm the current birth-year rules, format and registration path before acting.

01

League position

The current official name is U18 Nationell herr (historically referred to as J18 Nationell). It is a national U18 continuation stage with 24 teams divided into North and South groups.

02

2026/27 format, age and registration

The North and South groups play 22 rounds. The top seven teams in each group qualify for the U18 national playoffs; the bottom five move to U18 Regional competition. Birth-year eligibility, exceptions and registration deadlines remain season-specific.

03

Sporting assessment

Clubs assess skating, pace, skills, role, physical readiness, decision-making, full-game shifts and development potential, not highlights alone.

04

Foreign minors

Verify current SIF and IIHF conditions for an international transfer of a minor, including representation, education, safeguarding and any exceptional grounds.

05

School plan

Request the school, language, timetable, attendance, transport and responsible adult in writing. Education must remain workable if hockey plans change.

06

Housing and care

Clarify host family, boarding or parent accommodation, meals, room, curfew, insurance, transport, emergency contact and medical decision-making.

07

Recruitment timing

There is no universal tryout date. Ask whether the invitation concerns a real roster need, an assessment or a paid camp, and request a decision timeline.

08

Transfer and registration

A player last registered abroad may need an IIHF transfer and Swedish registration. Confirm who submits each document and when it must be complete.

09

Immigration and travel

Citizenship changes the administrative route. Check the official authority and consulate; selection does not itself grant permission to enter, study or register.

10

Costs and written terms

Ask for all fees, equipment, housing, travel and refund conditions in writing. Never pay an unknown person for an unverified guaranteed place.

11

Player package

Prepare year of birth, position, citizenship, current team, statistics, profile links, highlights and a full game, plus school and housing needs.
