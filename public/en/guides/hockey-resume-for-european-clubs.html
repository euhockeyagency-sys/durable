The Perfect Hockey Resume & Email Template for European GMs (Free Copy-Paste)

A one-page resume, a short cover email, subject lines and a follow-up, ready to copy or download. No sign-up needed.

Short answer

A European general manager decides in under a minute whether to open your video. Give them a one-page resume with the facts they need, birth year, position and shot, size with units, citizenship, current league, sourced statistics and working video links, and a short email that says who you are, what you want and how to reach you.

Below are free templates you can copy straight from this page or download as a text file. No sign-up is needed. Fill every [bracketed] field with your own verified information; do not invent achievements, recommendations or club interest.

1. What a GM checks first

Birth year and position, then size, citizenship (it decides import status), current league and recent statistics. Only if those fit do they open the video. Keep the resume to one page and the email to a few short paragraphs.

4. Cover email

Cover email
Subject: [Position], born [year], [current league] — profile for [season]

Dear [name, or "Hockey Operations"],

My name is [full name]. I am a [position] born in [year], shooting [left / right], [height] cm and [weight] kg. I currently play for [team] in [league].

Last season I played [GP] games and recorded [G] goals and [A] assists (source: [Elite Prospects / league website]). I am looking for an opportunity for the [season] season and I am available from [date].

Highlights: [link]
Full game: [link]
Elite Prospects: [link]
My one-page resume is attached as a PDF.

If my profile could fit your roster, I would be glad to send more video or answer any questions.

Kind regards,
[Full name]
[Email] | [Phone or WhatsApp]
[Players under 18: parent or guardian — name and contact]

Send it to the club's general contact or hockey operations address published on its official website. Personalise the first line for each club; a mass email with dozens of clubs in copy is usually ignored.

Send one follow-up after one to two weeks, in the same thread. If the club says no, thank them and move on.

7. What not to send in a first contact

  • copies of your passport or ID, or ID numbers;
  • your home address;
  • medical records;
  • for a minor: the player's personal phone, email or social accounts — use a parent's or guardian's contact.

A club that wants to sign you will ask for documents at the contract stage, through a verified channel.

8. Checklist before sending

  • Name, birth year, position, shot, height and weight with units, citizenship.
  • Current team and league with official names.
  • Statistics with season and source, matching Elite Prospects.
  • Highlights and full-game links open without login, checked on a phone.
  • Goal, availability and contract status stated.
  • Contact of the player or authorised representative; for under-18s, a parent or guardian.
  • One page, saved as PDF with a clear file name.
  • Email personalised for the club; no invented achievements or interest.

Common mistakes

  • Two or three pages of biography instead of current facts.
  • Statistics that conflict with Elite Prospects.
  • "I can play anywhere" instead of one main position.
  • Old youth achievements instead of recent seasons.
  • Unrealistic league targets; see choosing a realistic level.

Video matters as much as the resume: see how to make a highlight video, and plan the timing with the signing windows calendar.

FAQ

Is the template really free?

Yes. You can copy it from this page or download it without filling in any form.

Should I write my resume in English?

Yes. English is the working language across European markets. A correct local-language version can help in one country, but keep an English version.

Should I include my passport?

No, not in a first contact. State your citizenship; clubs ask for documents later, at the contract stage.

How many clubs should I email at once?

Write to clubs that fit your level and position, one email per club, personalised. Mass emails with many clubs in copy are usually ignored.

When should I follow up?

Once, after one to two weeks, in the same thread. If the club says no, thank them and move on.
