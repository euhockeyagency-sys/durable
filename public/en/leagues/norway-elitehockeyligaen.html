Elitehockeyligaen: player route and rules 2026/27

A verified player guide to Norway’s top senior competition: official 2026/27 schedule, recruitment, contracts, immigration and transfers.

01

League position and verified status

Elitehockeyligaen is Norway’s top national senior competition. Norges Ishockeyforbund lists the league under its published 2026/27 series schedules. Club participation and licensing must still be checked in the live federation system before relying on a roster opening.

02

2026/27 competition framework

The federation announced on 30 June 2026 that the Elitehockeyligaen schedule was published in HockeyLive. Match dates can change; use the live schedule and current competition rules rather than copying an old season’s team count or format.

03

Clubs and choosing a target

Target clubs by position, role, roster construction, import history, city and authorised sporting contact. Norway’s top level demands evidence that the player can contribute at professional pace. Verify the club and sender before sharing sensitive documents or paying travel costs.

04

What clubs assess

Clubs compare skating pace, decisions under pressure, special-teams value, role, verified recent statistics, full-game video, health, contract status, citizenship and administrative readiness. The application must show value at this exact level, not only career totals.

05

Roster and foreign-player checks

No universal import limit is safe to publish without the current competition rules and the player’s exact status. The club and federation must confirm in writing how citizenship, registration history, age and roster rules apply on the intended match date.

06

Recruitment timing and tryouts

Recruitment can continue before and during the season, but a listed club is not evidence of an opening. Confirm who invited the player, dates, evaluation criteria, expenses, insurance, accommodation and the decision deadline. A trial is not a contract.

07

Contract, pay and housing

There is no official universal salary table for every club. Put gross or net pay, payment dates, taxes, bonuses, housing, deposit, utilities, meals, travel, equipment, insurance, injury protection, trial status, termination and return travel in writing.

08

Residence and right to work

EU/EEA and third-country citizens follow different immigration and employment routes. A club invitation or sports agreement does not by itself grant entry, residence or work permission. Verify the official route for the player and club before travel.

09

IIHF transfer, registration and insurance

A move between national associations follows current IIHF transfer rules. The international transfer does not replace national registration, competition eligibility, insurance, immigration permission or a valid agreement. Confirm responsibility for every step and fee.

10

Player application package

Send passport and any second citizenship; birth year, position, shot, height and weight; current team; verified statistics; Elite Prospects or federation profile; role-based highlights and a recent full game; contract or release status; injury status and exact availability.

What level is Elitehockeyligaen?

It is Norway’s top national senior ice hockey competition.

Is the 2026/27 schedule published?

Yes. The Norwegian federation announced its publication in HockeyLive on 30 June 2026.

Is there one import limit for every player?

Do not assume one. The current rules and the player’s exact status must be checked.

Does a club offer grant work permission?

No. Immigration and employment eligibility are separate checks.
