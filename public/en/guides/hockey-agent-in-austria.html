EHA services · Austria

Hockey agent in Austria: player assessment and club search

EHA helps players and parents assess a route into Austrian hockey, prepare a clear profile and discuss the next steps. Club search and representation are agreed according to the player’s level, goals and practical needs.

Request an EHA consultation

Who this service is for

EHA assessment: the starting point is the player’s profile and goal. An established senior player needs a credible role; a junior moving abroad also needs a workable school, accommodation and care plan. A family with an invitation may need help comparing the sporting opportunity and written conditions.

EHA works on the Austrian market without presenting itself as an office based in Austria. Selection and an offer remain decisions for the receiving organisation.

Choose a realistic route before a club search

Use the Austria country guide for the system overview and practical preparation. The ICE Hockey League guide and Alps Hockey League guide explain the senior competitions. For a younger player, start with Austria junior hockey.

EHA assessment: the suitable target depends on recent competition, age, role, game video and readiness for the particular environment. A league name or points total alone does not establish a match.

How work with EHA proceeds

  1. Initial enquiry: send the current profile, recent video, target season and main questions.
  2. Clarify the starting point: review the stated level, goals, availability and missing information. A deeper video assessment can be a separate agreed service.
  3. Agree the scope: establish which work is included, responsibilities, any fees, payment terms and any representation or exclusivity terms before further work.
  4. Prepare and approach: where agreed, refine the presentation, assess relevant targets and handle club communication through EHA.
  5. Review a concrete next step: clarify a trial or offer, costs and the registration and residence checks required before travel.

Submitting a profile does not by itself start representation or confirm that EHA will approach clubs. The next action depends on the review and the agreed scope.

What the initial review can establish

The useful outcome is a proposed next step: clarify missing information, consider a more suitable level, prepare better game video, discuss a detailed assessment or consider a club-search service. An initial enquiry is not automatically a full scouting report or a confirmed shortlist.

For a more detailed sporting review, see EHA player assessment. Examples of previous work are available in the published cases; they do not predict another player’s result.

Support for junior players and parents

EHA assessment: judge the move by development and daily life together. Before a commitment, clarify the exact team, training group, competition for minutes, school admission and language, housing, responsible adult, meals, transport and full-season budget.

The family should understand what happens after non-selection, injury or an early departure and what any refund depends on. EHA can help identify questions and compare written conditions within the agreed service scope. See the parent guide for preparation.

Fees, representation and written conditions

Any further service, fee and payment terms are agreed separately before the work starts. Ask what is included: profile preparation, detailed video analysis, club search, communication, negotiation support or other assistance. Do not assume that one enquiry includes every stage.

If representation is proposed, clarify its duration, countries or competitions covered, exclusivity if any, authority to communicate, commission if applicable, termination and which expenses remain the player’s responsibility. A paid service is not a guarantee of a trial, contract, registration or permit.

What to send for an initial enquiry

  • birth year, position, shooting side, height, current team and league;
  • verifiable statistics and an Elite Prospects or federation profile if available;
  • recent full-game or full-shift video with jersey colour, number and timestamps;
  • citizenship, current country of residence, contract status and availability;
  • target season, desired level and practical expectations about housing and budget;
  • for a junior: school year, language level and the family’s accommodation and care needs.

A passport scan or medical file is not needed for the initial enquiry. Use the resume guide and video guide to make the profile clear.

Frequently asked questions

Can EHA guarantee a club in Austria?

No. The receiving organisation decides on selection and offers. Representation, payment or a trial does not guarantee a contract, registration or permit.

What happens after I submit a profile?

EHA reviews the enquiry and can clarify missing information or propose a next step. Representation and club approaches begin only within an agreed service scope.

Is a detailed video assessment included in the first enquiry?

A first enquiry is not automatically a full scouting report. A detailed assessment and its terms are agreed separately.

How are EHA fees agreed?

The scope, any fees, payment terms and representation conditions are agreed before the corresponding work starts. No standard fee is quoted on this page.

Can parents enquire for a junior player?

Yes. Include the playing profile, school year, language level, intended season, housing needs and approximate budget.

Do I need to send a passport scan?

No. State citizenship and country of residence for the initial enquiry; a passport scan and medical file are not needed at this stage.
